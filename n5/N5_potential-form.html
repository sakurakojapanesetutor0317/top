N5 GRAMMAR · VERB CONJUGATION

Potential Form 可能形かのうけい

Learn how to say what you can do in Japanese.

Learn to change ます-form verbs into dictionary form, use them in casual conversation, and connect them to essential grammar patterns.

N5VerbsDictionary form · Plain form

01 What Is the Potential Form?

The potential form expresses ability or possibility: what someone can do, or what circumstances make possible. It is a verb conjugation, so you can use it in both casual and polite sentences.

日本語にほんごが話はなせます。
I can speak Japanese.
今日きょうは会あえない。
I cannot meet today. (casual)

The potential form is different from permission (〜てもいいです). “Can do” does not always mean “may do.”

02 How to Make the Potential Form — Start with ます

Start with the ます-form, just as in the other verb-conjugation lessons.

Group 1 — Change the sound before ます to its え-row sound

Remove ます, change the last い-row sound to its え-row sound, then add ます for the polite potential form (き→け, ぎ→げ, し→せ, ち→て, に→ね, び→べ, み→め, り→れ, い→え).

書かきます → 書かけます
can write
話はなします → 話はなせます
can speak

Group 2 — Remove ます and add られます

食たべます → 食たべられます
can eat
見みます → 見みられます
can see / watch

Group 3 — Irregular verbs

します → できます
can do
来きます → 来こられます
can come

Watch the readings: 来きます → 来こられます → 来こられる. The potential of します is できます, not しられます.

03 Polite and Plain Potential Forms

The potential form itself has polite and plain versions. Use the plain form in casual speech and before many grammar patterns.

読よめます。
I can read it. (polite)
読よめる？
Can you read it? (casual question)
読よめません。 / 読よめない。
I cannot read it. (polite / casual)
読よめました。 / 読よめた。
I was able to read it. (polite / casual)

For Group 2, the plain potential ends in 〜られる; for Group 1, it ends in 〜える (for example, 話はなせる).

04 Potential Form vs. 〜ことができます

Both express ability. Dictionary form + ことができます is especially useful for beginners and in explanatory statements. Potential verbs are often shorter and natural in everyday conversation. They are not interchangeable in every context.

日本語にほんごを話はなすことができます。
I can speak Japanese. (dictionary form + ことができます)
日本語にほんごが話はなせます。
I can speak Japanese. (potential verb)

Use こと in the fixed ability pattern 〜ことができます; do not substitute の to make 〜のができます for this meaning.

05 Particles — が and を

When an object is expressed with a potential verb, が is very common, particularly in beginner examples. However, を is also possible with many potential verbs in modern Japanese. Do not treat “を is always wrong” as a rule.

漢字かんじを読よみます。
I read kanji. (ordinary action)
漢字かんじが読よめます。
I can read kanji. (common potential pattern)
漢字かんじを読よめます。
I can read kanji. (also used)

For early practice, start with が for the thing you can do or understand. Later, notice how を also appears in real speech and writing.

06 〜られる and Casual ら-Dropping

Group 2 potential verbs normally use 〜られる. In casual speech, many speakers drop ら, producing forms such as 〜れる. This is called ら-nuki kotoba (ら抜ぬき言葉ことば).

食たべられる → 食たべれる
can eat — standard → common casual form
見みられる → 見みれる
can see — standard → common casual form
来こられる → 来これる
can come — standard → common casual form

For textbooks, tests, and formal writing, use the standard 〜られる forms. In everyday conversation, you will hear ら-dropped forms often.

07 Potential Form vs. Passive Form

For Group 2 verbs and 来くる, potential and passive forms look the same. Use the particles and context to understand the meaning. Group 1 potential and passive forms are different.

私わたしはこの映画えいがが見みられます。
I can watch this movie. (potential)
私わたしは先生せんせいに見みられました。
I was seen by the teacher. (passive)
書かける / 書かかれる
can write / be written (Group 1: different forms)

The potential of 見みる and its passive form are both 見みられる. Meaning comes from the whole sentence, not the ending alone.

08 できる vs. 分わかる

できる describes being able to do something or completing a task. 分わかる means to understand. Understanding something is not always the same as being able to perform it.

日本語にほんごが分わかります。
I understand Japanese.
日本語にほんごが話はなせます。
I can speak Japanese.
料理りょうりができます。
I can cook. / The food is ready. (depending on context)

Do not mechanically change 分わかります into a potential form for “can understand.” 分わかります already expresses understanding.

09 When Ability Depends on the Situation

The potential form can describe not only a learned skill but also whether circumstances allow an action.

明日あしたは会あえます。
I can meet tomorrow. (availability)
ここから富士山ふじさんが見みえます。
You can see Mt. Fuji from here. (natural visibility)
音おとが聞きこえます。
I can hear a sound. (natural audibility)

見みえる and 聞きこえる often describe something being naturally visible or audible. Compare 見みられる and 聞きける, which can express ability or opportunity.

10 More Grammar with Potential Verbs

Potential verbs can be used with other conjugations and grammar patterns. These are examples of why learning the potential form is useful beyond a single sentence.

日本語にほんごが話はなせるようになりました。
I became able to speak Japanese.
明日あした、来こられたら連絡れんらくしてください。
If you can come tomorrow, please contact me.
全部ぜんぶは食たべられないかもしれません。
I might not be able to eat all of it.

Related grammar such as 〜ようになる, 〜たら, and 〜かもしれない can be studied in more detail on their own pages.
