Two ways to say your name

Compare the Japanese, romaji and English. The underlined part is what’s different.

1Most commonSimple & naturalTells people who you are. Use it in everyday introductions.
わたしWatashiはwaさくらこSakurakoです。desu.

I’m Sakurako.

2Says “my name”Names your name clearly. A little more formal and longer.
わたしWatashiのnoなまえnamaeはwaさくらこSakurakoです。desu.

My name is Sakurako.

のno
わたしwatashiのnoなまえnamae
→my name

の (no) links two words and shows who something belongs to, like English ’s or of. Both sentences are correct — ① is shorter and more natural.

!The particle は is read wa, not “ha”.
