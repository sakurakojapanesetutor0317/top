Sakura Japanese

Let’s practice Japanese!

Four short games to review your trial lesson. Every Japanese word has romaji underneath.

Listening

Listen, then choose the picture, the same sound or the sentence.

Conversation

Chat with Mika and choose a natural reply.

Flashcards

Review 20 phrases with pictures and audio.

Speaking

Listen, record your voice and compare.

Practice a little, whenever you like ✿

How to Play

Choose a game and tap Start. Each round has 10 questions or cards.

Listening

Tap the audio button, listen carefully, and choose the matching picture, sound, or sentence. Tap Check to see your answer.

Conversation

Read the situation and choose what you would say in Japanese. Sometimes you reply to Mika; sometimes you speak first.

Flashcards

Look at the picture and hint. Try saying the Japanese phrase, then tap the card to flip it. Listen to the audio and move to the next card.

Speaking

Listen to the example, record yourself with the microphone button, and play both recordings to compare. Allow microphone access if your browser asks. You can still listen and repeat without recording.
