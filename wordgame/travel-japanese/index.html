How to Play

  1. Choose a place (Restaurant, Café …) and a scene, such as Ordering or Paying.
  2. Study. Read the Culture Tips, then the Vocabulary, Customer (what you say) and Staff (what you will hear) lists — with romaji and English. Tap to listen.
  3. Play Vocabulary Rush. Choose Vocabulary, Phrase or Mixed practice. Japanese falls down the screen — tap the English meaning before it reaches the bottom. Questions come from every scene of the place you chose.
  4. Combo & score. Correct answers in a row raise your multiplier (×1.5, ×2, ×3). Faster answers score more.
  5. Master & review. Answer the same item correctly 3 times in a row to master it . Missed items go to To Review.

On a computer: press 1–4 to answer and Esc to pause.

Reset progress?

All mastered items and learning records on this device will be deleted. This can’t be undone.
