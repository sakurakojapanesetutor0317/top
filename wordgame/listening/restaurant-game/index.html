How to Play

  1. Choose a scene. Eight restaurant scenes, from entering to leaving.
  2. Study. Read the culture tips, then check the Vocabulary, what you say (Customer) and what staff say (Staff) — with romaji and English.
  3. Choose one practice category. Select Vocabulary (individual words), Customer (phrases you say as a customer), or Staff (phrases restaurant staff say). The default is Vocabulary. Categories are practised separately: both the questions and the English answer choices stay within your chosen category and scene.
  4. Play. Check the practice set, then start the game. A Japanese word or phrase falls down the screen — tap its English meaning before it reaches the bottom.
  5. Combo & score. Correct answers in a row raise your multiplier (×1.5, ×2, ×3). Faster answers score more.
  6. Master & review. Answer the same item correctly 3 times in a row to master it . Missed items go to To Review in that scene.

On a computer: press 1–4 to answer and Esc to pause.

Reset progress?

All mastered items and learning records on this device will be deleted. This can’t be undone.
