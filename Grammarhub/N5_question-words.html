Question Words

Who, what, where, when, and how — including when to read 何なに／なん as なに or なん.

01 Choose the information you need

Question words replace the information you do not know: a person, a place, a time, a thing, or an amount. Choose the word first, then keep the particle needed by the sentence.

Question wordAsk aboutExample
何なに / 何なんwhatこれは何なんですか。
What is this?
だれ / どなたwho · どなた is more politeあの人ひとはだれですか。
Who is that person?
どこ / どちらwhere · どちら is more polite for placesトイレはどこですか。
Where is the restroom?
いつwhenいつ日本にほんに行いきますか。
When will you go to Japan?
どれ / どの＋nounwhich one / which + nounどの本ほんですか。
Which book is it?
どう / いかがhow · いかが is more polite旅行りょこうはどうでしたか。
How was your trip?
どんな＋nounwhat kind of + nounどんな映画えいがが好すきですか。
What kind of films do you like?
どうして / なぜ / なんでwhyどうして日本語にほんごを勉強べんきょうしていますか。
Why are you studying Japanese?
いくらhow much · priceこれはいくらですか。
How much is this?
いくつ / 何なん＋counterhow manyりんごはいくつありますか。
How many apples are there?

02 Keep Japanese word order

Do not move a question word to the beginning just because English does. Put it where the answer would go. Add か to make a polite question; the polite verb or です stays at the end.

明日あした、図書館としょかんで勉強べんきょうします。

I will study at the library tomorrow.

明日あした、どこで勉強べんきょうしますか。

Where will you study tomorrow?

これは何なんですか。

What is this?

One question word does not need another question word

You do not need to add 何なに／なん to words such as どこ or いつ. Each already asks for the missing information.

03 Question words + particles

Particles show the role of the missing information. Compare the questions with their answers.

QuestionMeaningPossible answer
だれが来きますか。Who is coming?田中たなかさんが来きます。
だれと行いきますか。Who will you go with?友達ともだちと行いきます。
だれに聞ききましたか。Who did you ask?先生せんせいに聞ききました。
どこに住すんでいますか。Where do you live?東京とうきょうに住すんでいます。
どこで勉強べんきょうしますか。Where will you study?図書館としょかんで勉強べんきょうします。
何なにを買かいましたか。What did you buy?本ほんを買かいました。
何なにが好すきですか。What do you like?映画えいがが好すきです。
だれのかばんですか。Whose bag is it?私わたしのかばんです。
だれが, rather than だれは, for “Who…?”

Use が when asking which person is the subject: だれが来きますか。 The unknown person is the focus. This does not mean は is forbidden everywhere in a question: これは何なんですか。 is a normal question about an established topic.

04 どれ・どの・どちら・どんな

どれ stands alone. どの and どんな need a noun immediately after them.

FormUseExample
どれWhich one? Usually a choice from three or more.どれがあなたのかばんですか。
Which one is your bag?
どの＋nounWhich particular member of a set?どのかばんがあなたのですか。
Which bag is yours?
どちらWhich of two? Also a polite expression for direction or place.コーヒーと紅茶こうちゃと、どちらがいいですか。
Which would you like, coffee or tea?
どんな＋nounWhat kind? Ask for a type or description.どんなかばんが欲ほしいですか。
What kind of bag do you want?
Different questions, different answers

どの映画えいが？ asks you to identify a film, for example by its title. どんな映画えいが？ asks about its kind or characteristics, such as a comedy or a funny film.

05 When, how, and why

いつ vs. a specific time question

いつ日本にほんに行いきますか。

When will you go to Japan? → A day, month, season, etc. can answer this.

何時なんじに出でかけますか。

What time will you go out? → Ask specifically for a clock time.

Use いつ without に in an ordinary “when” question. With a clock time, use 何時なんじに. いつから (from when) and いつまで (until when) ask about the beginning and end of a period.

どう vs. どうやって

日本にほんの生活せいかつはどうですか。

How is life in Japan? → Ask for an impression or evaluation.

駅えきまでどうやって行いきますか。

How do you get to the station? → Ask for the method or route.

どうして・なぜ・なんで

All can ask “why.” どうして is common in conversation, なぜ often sounds more formal or analytical, and なんで is conversational. A direct “Why?” can sound demanding depending on tone and context.

どうして日本語にほんごを勉強べんきょうしていますか。

Why are you studying Japanese?

日本にほんで働はたらきたいからです。

Because I want to work in Japan.

06 何なに／なん — How do you choose?

The kanji 何 means “what,” but it has two common pronunciations: なに and なん. The reading often depends on the sound or word that follows and whether you are asking for a number or quantity. These are useful patterns, not rules without exceptions.

Quick guide: なに vs. なん

なに — on its own, or commonly before を・が・に.

なん — commonly before です・だ・の, and in many number/counter expressions.

Learn the complete expression when a reading does not follow these patterns.

1. The following sound or particle

ReadingCommon patternExample
なにOn its own何なに？ What?
なにBefore を・が・に何なにを食たべますか。
What will you eat?
なんBefore です・だこれは何なんですか。
What is this?
なんBefore の何なんの本ほんですか。
What kind of book is it?
なんOften before と何なんと言いいましたか。
What did they say?

Pronunciation tip: なん often appears before sounds beginning with D (です・だ), T (と), or N (の). These are common patterns, not absolute rules. For example, 何なにと is also used in some expressions.

2. Numbers and counters: usually なん

When asking “how many,” “what time,” or another numerical question, なん is the usual reading before many counters.

ExpressionMeaningExpressionMeaning
何人なんにんHow many people?何枚なんまいHow many flat items?
何個なんこHow many pieces?何歳なんさいHow old?
何時なんじWhat time?何分なんぷんHow many minutes?
何時間なんじかんHow many hours?何月なんがつWhat month?
But 何 + kanji does NOT always mean なん!

何色なにいろ (what color), 何語なにご (what language), and 何味なにあじ (what flavor) use なに. These ask about a kind or category rather than a number.

3. The same writing can have different meanings

何人なんにんいますか。

How many people are there?

あの人ひとは何人なにじんですか。

What nationality is that person?

何なにで行いきますか。

How will you get there? (By what means?)

なんで行いくんですか。

Why are you going?

In everyday speech, なんで can also ask about a means of transport. Context matters; どうやって (how) and どうして (why) can make the distinction clearer.

07 Same writing, different question

何人なんにん／なにじん: how many people, or what nationality?

パーティーに何人なんにん来きますか。

How many people are coming to the party? → A number, such as three.

あの人ひとは何人なにじんですか。

What nationality is that person? → A nationality, such as Canadian.

何人なんにん counts people. 何人なにじん asks about nationality. The same written characters do not guarantee the same reading. Asking どちらの国くにの方かたですか。 is a more polite option when nationality is relevant.

何語なにご／なんご: which language, or how many words?

何語なにごを話はなしますか。

What language(s) do you speak?

何語なにご asks which language. 何語なんご can ask for a word count in a context about counting words. Do not assume every 何なに／なん + kanji is a counting question.

何なに／なんで: means or reason?

何なにで駅えきまで行いきますか。

By what means will you get to the station? → By bus, by train, etc.

なんで駅えきまで行いくんですか。

Why are you going to the station? → Ask for a reason.

なんで is not always “why”

For means, なにで is a clear learner-friendly reading, but なんで is also used in everyday speech. Pronunciation alone does not always settle the meaning. Listen to context. To make the question clearer, use どうやって for a method or どうして for a reason.

08 Amounts, counters, and duration

Ask aboutQuestionAnswer example
priceいくらですか。1,000円えんです。
general number of thingsいくつありますか。3つあります。
number of people何人なんにんいますか。3人にんいます。
number of flat things何枚なんまいありますか。3枚まいあります。
clock time何時なんじですか。3時じです。
length of time何時間なんじかんかかりますか。3時間じかんかかります。
duration in generalどのくらいかかりますか。3時間じかんぐらいかかります。
age何歳なんさいですか。 / おいくつですか。20歳さいです。

Use the counter that matches the thing you count. 何日なんにち can ask for a date or a number of days; the rest of the sentence tells you which:

今日きょうは何月なんがつ何日なんにちですか。

What is today’s date?

日本にほんに何日なんにちいますか。

How many days will you be in Japan?

いくつ is a general question for countable things, but it does not replace every counter. For people, use 何人なんにん. おいくつですか is a polite way to ask age, though age can still be personal information.

09 Question word + か / も

The same words can appear in statements. Question word + か often means “some…”. Question word + も + a negative predicate often means “no…” or “not any…”. These are different from sentence-final か, which marks a question.

QuestionSome…No… + negative
何なに · what何なにか · something何なにも · nothing
だれ · whoだれか · someoneだれも · no one
どこ · whereどこか · somewhereどこにも / どこへも · nowhere (with movement / existence)

何なにか食たべたいです。

I want to eat something.

今日きょうは何なにも食たべていません。

I have not eaten anything today.

部屋へやにはだれもいません。

There is no one in the room.

昨日きのうはどこにも行いきませんでした。

I did not go anywhere yesterday.

Keep particles when they are needed

だれかに聞ききます。 means “I will ask someone.” だれにも聞ききません。 means “I will not ask anyone.” With this negative pattern, も follows に. Do not assume that か or も always replaces the original particle.
