Basic Particles

Start with words and simple sentence patterns. Learn the common expressions with が, and don't worry about mastering every particle rule yet!

N5ParticlesBasic Sentence Patterns

01 は — Basic Sentence Pattern

Use は to introduce what you are talking about. For now, learn it as part of a basic sentence pattern.

A は B です。 / A は ... Verb.

Example sentences

わたしは学生がくせいです。

I am a student.

わたしはコーヒーを飲のみます。

I drink coffee.

は is written as ha but pronounced wa when used as a particle.

02 が — Common Expressions

At N5, focus on remembering these common patterns with が.

Noun + が + expression
〜が好すきですlike
〜が嫌きらいですdislike
〜が上手じょうずですbe good at
〜が下手へたですbe bad at
〜がわかりますunderstand
〜がありますthere is / have (things)
〜がいますthere is / have (people and animals)
〜がほしいですwant something
〜ができますcan do / be able to do

Example sentences

ねこが好すきです。

I like cats.

日本語にほんごがわかります。

I understand Japanese.

時間じかんがありません。

I don't have time.

新あたらしいかばんがほしいです。

I want a new bag.

These are useful patterns to memorize, not a complete list of every use of が.

03 を — Object Marker

を marks the object of an action. It is written as wo in romaji but usually pronounced o.

Noun + を + Verb

Example sentences

パンを食たべます。

I eat bread.

映画えいがを見みます。

I watch a movie.

In conversation, を is often omitted!

In everyday speech, people often leave out を when the meaning is clear. You can usually still be understood.

パンを食たべます。I eat bread.

パン食たべます。I eat bread. (common in conversation)

Learn the standard pattern for writing and careful speech, but don't let a missing を stop you from speaking!

04 Important Notes

は vs が — Think of “a” and “the” in English!

In English, we use a and the differently depending on what the listener already knows.

Japanese は and が can work in a similar way.

  • が — often introduces new information.
  • は — often talks about something already known or established.

This is just a simple way to understand the difference. The rules are not exactly the same.

At N5, you don't need to worry too much about this! Focus on learning common patterns with が first.
