N5 GRAMMAR · PARTICLES

と・や・も・の

And · With · Also · Possession

Learn how to connect nouns, talk about doing things with someone, add information, and show possession.

N5ParticlesConnecting · Adding · Possession

01と — And (Complete List)

Use と to connect nouns when you are listing the items together. It does not connect full sentences.

Noun A + と + Noun B

Example sentences

パンとたまごを食たべます。

I eat bread and eggs.

月曜日げつようびと水曜日すいようびに勉強べんきょうします。

I study on Monday and Wednesday.

02と — With Someone

Use と after a person to say you do something with them.

Person + と + Verb

Example sentences

友達ともだちと映画えいがを見みます。

I watch a movie with a friend.

母ははと買かい物ものに行いきます。

I go shopping with my mother.

03や — And (Some Examples)

Use や to list some examples, not necessarily everything. など means “etc.” and is often added at the end.

Noun A + や + Noun B + (など)

Example sentences

りんごやバナナを買かいました。

I bought apples, bananas, and other things.

本ほんやノートなどがあります。

There are books, notebooks, etc.

と vs や — What is the difference?

と = A and B (a complete list in context). や = A, B, and other things (examples).

04も — Also / Too

Use も when adding something that is also true. It often replaces は, が, or を.

Noun + も + Predicate

Example sentences

わたしも学生がくせいです。

I am a student, too.

コーヒーも飲のみます。

I drink coffee, too.

日本語にほんごも英語えいごも話はなします。

I speak both Japanese and English.

Important: も and other particles

も often replaces は, が, or を, but it can follow に or で: 学校がっこうにも / 学校がっこうでも.

05の — Possession, Description & Noun Replacement

Use の to connect nouns, show possession, or replace a noun when its meaning is clear from context.

Noun A + の + Noun B

Example sentences — Possession & relationships

これはわたしの本ほんです。

This is my book. (possession)

日本語にほんごの先生せんせいです。

(Someone) is a Japanese teacher. (relationship)

日本語にほんごの本ほんです。

It's a Japanese-language book. (type / content)

東京とうきょうのホテルに泊とまります。

I stay at a hotel in Tokyo. (location / relationship)

机つくえの上うえに本ほんがあります。

There is a book on the desk. (position)

Example sentences — Questions with の

これは何なんの本ほんですか。

What kind of book is this?

これはだれの本ほんですか。

Whose book is this?

の can replace a noun

When the noun is already clear, you can leave it out and use の instead.

Adjective + の / Noun + の

Example sentences

A: これはだれの本ほんですか。
B: わたしのです。

A: Whose book is this?
B: It's mine.

赤あかいのをください。

Please give me the red one.

この大おおきいのがほしいです。

I want this big one.
