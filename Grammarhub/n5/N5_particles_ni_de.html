N5 GRAMMAR · PARTICLES

に・で

Time, Place & More

Learn where, when, and how to use に and で. Start with the most useful patterns, then practice the difference!

N5ParticlesTime · Place · Means

01に — Time

Use に with specific times and dates to say when something happens.

Time + に + Verb

Example sentences

7時じに起おきます。

I wake up at 7.

月曜日げつようびに学校がっこうへ行いきます。

I go to school on Monday.

When do we use に with time?

Use に for specific clock times and calendar dates. Usually, do not use it with relative time words such as “today,” “tomorrow,” or “last year.”

Days of the week: Both 月曜日げつようびに行きいます and 月曜日げつようび行きいます are possible. For beginners, using に is an easy pattern to learn.

Example sentences

7時しちじに起きおます。

I wake up at 7.

明日あした、学校がっこうに行きいます。

I'll go to school tomorrow.

去年きょねん、日本にほんに行きいました。

I went to Japan last year.

毎日まいにち、日本語にほんごを勉強べんきょうします。

I study Japanese every day.

The に in 学校がっこうに / 日本にほんに marks a destination, not time.

02に — Destination

Use に for the destination or arrival point of movement.

Destination + に + 行いきます / 来きます / 帰かえります

Example sentences

学校がっこうに行いきます。

I go to school.

うちに帰かえります。

I go home.

With 行きいます・来きます・帰りかえます, へ can often be used instead of に to indicate direction.

03に — Where Something Exists

Use に for the location of a person or thing with あります / います.

Place + に + Noun + が + あります / います

Example sentences

部屋へやにねこがいます。

There is a cat in the room.

つくえの上うえに本ほんがあります。

There is a book on the desk.

04More Uses of に

You'll also see に in these common N5 patterns.

Recipient / Person — to

友達ともだちにプレゼントをあげます。

I give a present to my friend.

Purpose — to do something

映画えいがを見みに行いきます。

I go to watch a movie.

ます-stem + に + 行きいます / 来きます

Result of Change — become

先生せんせいになります。

I become a teacher.

Frequency — per

1週間しゅうかんに3回かい運動うんどうします。

I exercise three times a week.

05で — Place of Action

Use で to say where an action takes place.

Place + で + Action Verb

Example sentences

学校がっこうで勉強べんきょうします。

I study at school.

レストランで食たべます。

I eat at a restaurant.

06で — Means, Tools & Transport

Use で for how you do something: a tool, language, or means of transportation.

Tool / Means + で + Verb

Example sentences

バスで学校がっこうに行いきます。

I go to school by bus.

はしで食たべます。

I eat with chopsticks.

日本語にほんごで話はなします。

I speak in Japanese.

ペンで書かきます。

I write with a pen.

Exception to remember: 歩あるいて (on foot) normally doesn't use で.

07に vs で — Place

Both can follow a place, but they tell you different things. Focus on the verb!

に — destination / existence

学校がっこうに行いきます。

I go to school.

学校がっこうに先生せんせいがいます。

There is a teacher at school.

で — action location

学校がっこうで勉強べんきょうします。

I study at school.

学校がっこうで友達ともだちと話はなします。

I talk with a friend at school.

Remember the verb, not the English preposition!

English at / in / to does not match Japanese particles one-to-one. Learn the sentence pattern together with its verb.
