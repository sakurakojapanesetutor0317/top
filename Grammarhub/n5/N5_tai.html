N5 GRAMMAR · DESIRE

〜たい

Want to do something

Use 〜たい to talk about something you want to do. Attach it to the ます-stem of a verb.

N5Desire / WishVerb + たい
Masu-stem

01 Meaning & Usage

〜たい expresses the speaker's desire to do an action: “want to do ...”

It is commonly used to express your own wishes or to ask someone directly what they want to do.

Verb ます-stem + たいです

For example: 食たべます → 食たべたいです (want to eat)

02 Formation & Conjugation

Remove ます from the polite verb form and add たい.

行いきます → 行いきたいです ／ 見みます → 見みたいです ／ します → したいです

〜たい conjugates like an い-adjective.

Present食たべたいです — want to eat
Negative食たべたくないです — don't want to eat
Past食たべたかったです — wanted to eat
Past negative食たべたくなかったです — didn't want to eat

03 Example Sentences

日本にほんに行いきたいです。

I want to go to Japan.

今日きょうは何なにもしたくないです。

I don't want to do anything today.

昨日きのう、ラーメンを食たべたかったです。

I wanted to eat ramen yesterday.

週末しゅうまつ、何なにをしたいですか。

What do you want to do this weekend?

04 Important Notes

を / が with 〜たい

Both 水みずを飲のみたいです and 水みずが飲のみたいです are possible. The choice depends on the sentence and emphasis.

Talking about someone else's wishes

〜たい usually expresses your own wishes or is used in direct questions. For a third person's observed desire, 〜たがっています is a common alternative.

〜たい vs. 〜ほしい

〜たい = want to do something. 〜がほしい = want a thing.
