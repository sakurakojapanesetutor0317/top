N5 GRAMMAR · CONDITIONS & RESULTS

〜と・〜ても

When / If · Even If

Compare results triggered by a condition with results that remain true even under that condition.

N5Condition · Result〜と · 〜ても
Te-formAdjectiveNoun
〜と〜ても

01Two Different Relationships

〜と links a condition or trigger to a result that follows regularly or predictably. 〜ても says that the result or decision remains true even under that condition.

雨あめが 降ふると、試合しあいは 中止ちゅうしになります。
When it rains, the game is canceled. — a regular rule in this situation
雨あめが 降ふっても、試合しあいを します。
Even if it rains, we will play the game. — rain does not change the decision
Read the second half carefully

AとB: A triggers B.
AてもB: even with A, B still holds.

ても often goes against an expected result, but it does not always express surprise. It can simply say a condition does not prevent the result.

02〜と — How to Connect It

For verbs, use the plain non-past form + と: dictionary form for an affirmative condition, ない-form for a negative condition. Affirmative non-past nouns and な-adjectives keep だ.

Type Connection Example
Verb · affirmative Dictionary form + と 押おすと
Verb · negative ない-form + と 押おさないと
い-adjective Plain non-past + と 高たかいと / 高たかくないと
な-adjective だ + と / negative + と 静しずかだと / 静しずかじゃないと
Noun だ + と / negative + と 休やすみだと / 休やすみじゃないと
Start from ます when forming the verb

押おします → 押おす → 押おすと
行いきます → 行いく → 行いくと
食たべます → 食たべない → 食たべないと

Do not add だ after an い-adjective or a verb: ✗ 高たかいだと / 押おすだと.

03Use と for Mechanisms, Directions, and Regular Results

と is especially useful for instructions about how something works, directions, natural results, and repeated habits. In these uses, it is closer to “when / whenever / if A, B follows” than to an open-ended personal choice.

この ボタンを 押おすと、ドアが 開あきます。
When you press this button, the door opens. — mechanism
この 道みちを まっすぐ 行いくと、右みぎに 駅えきが あります。
If you go straight along this road, the station is on your right. — directions
春はるに なると、暖あたたかく なります。
When spring comes, it gets warm. — seasonal change
朝あさごはんを 食たべないと、おなかが すきます。
If I do not eat breakfast, I get hungry. — regular result
休やすみだと、朝あさ 遅おそくまで 寝ねます。
When I have a day off, I sleep late. — habit
部屋へやが 静しずかだと、よく 眠ねむれます。
When the room is quiet, I can sleep well. — condition supporting a result
A negative condition is still a condition

食たべないと、おなかがすきます = not eating leads to hunger. The と does not make the negative verb positive.

The shortened expression もう行いかないと can mean “I have to go now,” with something like いけません left unsaid. That is a related obligation expression, not the basic full conditional used here.

04Do Not Use Conditional と for Every “If”

In its basic conditional use, と does not normally introduce a request, invitation, command, or a one-time intention as the result. Use an appropriate pattern such as たら for those situations.

✓ 駅えきに 着ついたら、電話でんわしてください。
✗ 駅えきに 着つくと、電話でんわしてください。
When you arrive at the station, please call me. — a request
✓ 時間じかんが あったら、一緒いっしょに 映画えいがを 見みませんか。
✗ 時間じかんが あると、一緒いっしょに 映画えいがを 見みませんか。
If you have time, would you like to watch a movie together? — invitation
この ボタンを 押おすと、音おとが 出でます。
If you press this button, a sound comes out. — predictable result
安やすかったら、この かばんを 買かいます。
If this bag is inexpensive, I will buy it. — a decision for this particular occasion
と can describe a discovery in a past story

家いえに帰かえると、友達ともだちがいました。
When I got home, my friend was there.

Here, と connects arrival with what the speaker found, rather than a universal rule. Learn the regular-result use first; do not turn “と always means an automatic result” into an absolute rule.

05〜ても — How to Connect It

Use the て-form + も. If the て-form ends in で, keep で and add も. For adjectives and nouns, use their connecting forms.

Type How to form it Example
Verb て-form + も 行いっても / 食たべても
Verb ending in で で + も 読よんでも / 飲のんでも
い-adjective い → くても 高たかくても
な-adjective Stem + でも 静しずかでも
Noun Noun + でも 雨あめでも
Negative ない → なくても 行いかなくても / 高たかくなくても
Make the て-form first

行いきます → 行いって → 行いっても
飲のみます → 飲のんで → 飲のんでも
高たかい → 高たかくて → 高たかくても

いい is irregular: いい → よくても. Do not use いくても.

06Even If / Even Though — What Stays True?

Even if is useful for a hypothetical condition; even though can fit an already known fact. Japanese ても can express either relationship depending on context.

雨あめが 降ふっても、行いきます。
Even if it rains, I will go. — a possible condition
高たかくても、この かばんを 買かいたいです。
Even if it is expensive, I want to buy this bag.
この 問題もんだいは、何度なんど 読よんでも 分わかりません。
No matter how many times I read this question, I do not understand it.
毎日まいにち 勉強べんきょうしても、まだ 分わからない ことが あります。
Even though I study every day, there are still things I do not understand.
休やすみでも、早はやく 起おきます。
Even on a day off, I get up early.
部屋へやが 静しずかでも、心配しんぱいな ことが あると 眠ねむれません。
Even if the room is quiet, I cannot sleep when something is worrying me.
ても can lead to a deliberate decision

雨あめが降ふっても、行いきます is natural: the speaker’s decision does not change. This is different from the restriction on requests and one-time intentions after basic conditional と.

07Keep These Similar-Looking Patterns Separate

The words following ても determine the complete expression. Do not assume every ても sentence simply means “even if.”

雨あめが 降ふっても、行いきます。
Even if it rains, I will go. — unchanged decision
ここで 写真しゃしんを 撮とってもいいですか。
May I take photos here? — てもいい: permission
明日あしたは 来こなくてもいいです。
You do not have to come tomorrow. — なくてもいい: not necessary
明日あしたは 来こなくても、大丈夫だいじょうぶです。
It is okay even if you do not come tomorrow. — negative condition + result
These are related, but the meaning of the full pattern matters

食たべてもいい = you may eat.
食たべなくてもいい = you do not have to eat.
食たべてはいけない = you must not eat.

Permission and prohibition are already covered in the て-form lesson. Here, focus on the condition/result relationship of ても.

08Compare the Same Condition

Change only the connector and follow the meaning of the result.

雨あめが 降ふると、道みちが ぬれます。
When it rains, the road gets wet. — a natural result
雨あめが 降ふっても、屋根やねの 下したの 道みちは ぬれません。
Even if it rains, the path under the roof does not get wet. — the roof prevents the expected result
この ボタンを 押おすと、ドアが 開あきます。
Pressing this button opens the door.
この ボタンを 押おしても、ドアが 開あきません。
Even if I press this button, the door does not open. — expected result fails to occur
A simple check

Does A regularly lead to B? Consider と.
Does B still hold despite A? Consider ても.
Do you want to request, invite, or state a particular decision if A happens? Consider another conditional such as たら.

09Conversation Examples

A：この ボタンを 押おすと、どう なりますか。
B：電気でんきが つきます。
A: What happens if I press this button?
B: The light comes on.
A：明日あした、雨あめが 降ふっても 行いきますか。
B：はい、行いきます。中なかで 遊あそべますから。
A: Will you go even if it rains tomorrow?
B: Yes. We can have fun indoors.
A：駅えきは どこですか。
B：この 道みちを まっすぐ 行いくと、左ひだりに あります。
A: Where is the station?
B: Go straight along this road, and it is on your left.
A：少すこし 高たかくても、大丈夫だいじょうぶですか。
B：はい。便利べんりな ほうが いいです。
A: Is it okay even if it is a little expensive?
B: Yes. I would prefer something convenient.
