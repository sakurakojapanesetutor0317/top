03

Lesson Focus

A short note for this chapter

Read the description together with its noun

［昨日きのう買かった］本ほん means “the book [I bought yesterday].” The description comes before 本ほん and identifies it. The noun phrase can then take を・が・は in the main sentence.

The dedicated page marks the modifier and head noun in both Japanese and English, then builds up to longer sentences.
