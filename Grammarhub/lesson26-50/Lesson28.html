SAKURA JAPANESE · N4

Lesson 28

Describe two actions happening together, talk about regular activities, and give several reasons or features.

01

Doing Two Things — 〜ながら

Two actions by the same person at the same time.

ます-stem + ながら, then the main action

Remove ます from the first verb and add ながら: 聞ききます → 聞ききながら. This is the same ます-stem you have used before.

The action after ながら is the main activity. Both actions are done by the same person and overlap in time. This is different from doing one action and then another with 〜てから.

Main activity: cooking

音楽おんがくを聞ききながら、料理りょうりをします。

I cook while listening to music.

Two ongoing activities

働はたらきながら、日本語にほんごを勉強べんきょうしています。

I am studying Japanese while working.

02

Regular Activities — 〜ています

An ongoing habit, not only something happening right now.

Look at the time expression and context

て-form + います can describe a regular activity or an ongoing habit. Expressions such as “every morning” and “on days off” help show this meaning. It does not necessarily mean the person is doing the activity at the moment of speaking.

For example, 毎朝まいあさ走はしっています describes a routine, while 今いま走はしっています describes what is happening now. The same form can have different meanings in context.

毎朝まいあさ、公園こうえんで走はしっています。

I go running in the park every morning.

休やすみの日ひは、絵えを描かいています。

I draw pictures on my days off.

03

Several Reasons or Features — 〜し、〜し

List supporting points without suggesting that these are the only ones.

Plain form + し

Attach し to a plain-form clause. You can list several qualities, give more than one reason, or add a reason before a conclusion. The list often suggests that there may be other points too.

Keep だ after a noun or な-adjective in the affirmative non-past: 学生がくせいだし・親切しんせつだし. Do not add だ to an い-adjective: 安やすいし. Verbs also use their plain forms: 行いくし・行いかないし・行いったし.

In polite conversation, ます / です + し is also possible. Here, focus on recognizing and practicing the plain-form connection from the lesson.

Reasons → conclusion

この店みせは安やすいし、おいしいし、よく来きます。

I often come to this restaurant because it is inexpensive and the food is good.

List positive features

田中たなかさんは親切しんせつだし、話はなしもおもしろいです。

Tanaka is kind, and what they say is interesting too.

Review Plain FormsCheck verb, adjective, and noun endings before adding し.→〜し / それでList reasons and connect them to a result.→
04

Lesson Focus

Choose what you want to connect.

Two actions, a habit, or several reasons?

〜ながら connects overlapping activities by the same person. Put the main activity at the end.

〜ています can describe a regular activity when the context makes it a habit.

〜し、〜し connects reasons or features. It does not mean that the actions happen at the same time.

With し, も often helps add another point: 値段ねだんも安やすいし、味あじもいいです — “The price is low, and the taste is good too.” Use も to match the meaning; it is not a required part of every し clause.
