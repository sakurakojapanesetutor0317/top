SAKURA JAPANESE · N4

Lesson 34

Describe how to do something, the order of actions, and what you do without doing something else.

01

Follow a Model — 〜とおりに

Do something as shown, said, or written.

Dictionary form / た-form + とおりに; noun + のとおりに

Use the dictionary form when the model or demonstration is still to come; use the た-form when it has already been shown, said, or written. A noun connects with の. The point is matching a model, not just doing two actions in sequence.

今いまから私わたしがやるとおりに、やってください。

Please do it the way I am about to demonstrate.

説明書せつめいしょのとおりに、組くみ立たてました。

I assembled it according to the instructions.

02

After — 〜あとで

Do a second action after the first.

Verb た-form + あとで; noun + のあとで

The first action is presented as complete before the next action. Use the た-form even for a future plan. A noun needs の: 仕事しごとのあとで.

宿題しゅくだいをしたあとで、映画えいがを見みます。

I will watch a film after doing my homework.

会議かいぎのあとで、少すこし話はなしませんか。

Shall we talk for a moment after the meeting?

03

With / Without an Action

Describe the manner or accompanying action.

Verb て-form / ない-form + で、verb

The て-form can describe what you do as part of another action. 〜ないで means without doing something, or choosing another action instead. Distinguish 食たべないで (“without eating”) from 食たべなくて (“because [someone] did not eat,” depending on context).

砂糖さとうを入いれて、コーヒーを飲のみます。

I drink coffee with sugar in it.

朝あさご飯はんを食たべないで、学校がっこうへ行いきました。

I went to school without eating breakfast.

04

Lesson Focus

Keep the meaning, connection, and viewpoint clear.

Points to remember

〜とおりに gives a model to follow. 〜あとで gives an order in time. 〜ないで describes doing something without another action; it is different from the reason pattern 〜なくて in Lesson 39.
