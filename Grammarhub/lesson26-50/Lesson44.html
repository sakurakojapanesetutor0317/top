SAKURA JAPANESE · N4

Lesson 44

Describe excess, ease and difficulty, and deliberate changes or choices.

01

Too Much — 〜すぎます

An action or quality exceeds a suitable amount.

Verb ます-stem / い-adjective without い / な-adjective stem + すぎます

すぎる conjugates as a Group 2 verb: すぎます / すぎました / すぎない. Connect to the stem, not the complete ます-form or the adjective with だ. With いい, use よすぎます.

昨日きのうは食たべすぎました。

I ate too much yesterday.

この靴くつは小ちいさすぎます。

These shoes are too small.

02

Easy or Difficult to Do

Describe ease, difficulty, or a tendency.

Verb ます-stem + やすい / にくい

These endings conjugate like い-adjectives. They can describe practical ease or difficulty, and sometimes how readily something happens: 壊こわれやすい means “breaks easily.” They do not simply mean that the person has or lacks the skill.

このペンは書かきやすいです。

This pen is easy to write with.

この字じは小ちいさくて、読よみにくいです。

These letters are small and hard to read.

03

Make It… / Choose… — 〜にします

Change something deliberately or settle on a choice.

い-adjective: 〜くします; な-adjective / noun: 〜にします

Use く for an い-adjective and に for a な-adjective or noun. With a noun, 〜にします can also express choosing an option: コーヒーにします. The context distinguishes a change from a choice.

音おとを小ちいさくしてください。

Please turn the volume down.

「お飲のみ物ものは？」「コーヒーにします。」

“What would you like to drink?” — “I will have coffee.”

04

Lesson Focus

Keep the meaning, connection, and viewpoint clear.

Points to remember

〜すぎる means more than is appropriate, not simply “very.” 〜やすい / 〜にくい attach to a verb stem. Compare deliberate 大おおきくします (“make it bigger”) with 大おおきくなります (“it becomes bigger”).
