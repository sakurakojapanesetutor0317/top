02 Choose the Right Connector — Meaning & Register

Words translated the same way in English are not always interchangeable in Japanese. Compare their purpose, tone, and position.

But / However — でも・だけど・ですが・しかし・ところが・それでも

ExpressionWhen to use itCompare in context
でもEveryday, neutral spoken contrast
今日きょうは雨あめです。でも、出でかけます。
It is raining. But I am going out.
だけどCasual, soft spoken contrast; also sentence-initial
今日きょうは雨あめだ。だけど、出でかける。
It is raining. But I am going out.
ですがPolite, somewhat formal contrast
今日きょうは雨あめです。ですが、出でかけます。
It is raining. However, I am going out.
しかしWritten/formal logical contrast
天候てんこうは悪化あっかしました。しかし、予定よていは変更へんこうされませんでした。
The weather worsened. However, the plan was not changed.
ところがUnexpected turn, contrary to expectation
晴はれると思おもいました。ところが、雨あめが降ふり始はじめました。
I thought it would clear up. Unexpectedly, it started raining.
それでもDespite an obstacle, action continues
雨あめが降ふっています。それでも、出でかけます。
It is raining. Even so, I am going out.

Adding Information — それと・それに・また・さらに・なお

ExpressionWhen to use itCompare in context
それとAdd another item or request, conversational
パンをください。それと、コーヒーもお願ねがいします。
Bread, please. And a coffee too.
それにAdd another supporting reason
この店みせは安やすいです。それに、おいしいです。
This place is cheap. Besides, it is delicious.
またNeutral additional information
駅えきに近ちかいです。また、駐車場ちゅうしゃじょうもあります。
It is near the station. There is also parking.
さらにDevelop or strengthen a point
値段ねだんが下さがりました。さらに、品質ひんしつも上あがりました。
The price fell. Furthermore, quality improved.
なおSeparate supplementary notice
受付うけつけは五時ごじまでです。なお、日曜にちようは休やすみです。
Reception closes at five. Please note we are closed Sundays.

Sequence — そして・それから・そのあと

ExpressionWhen to use itCompare in context
そしてConnects story events or information
朝あさごはんを食たべました。そして、学校がっこうへ行いきました。
I ate breakfast and then went to school.
それからHighlights the next action in time
宿題しゅくだいをしました。それから、寝ねました。
I did my homework. Then I went to bed.
そのあとPoints to the period after a specific event
映画えいがを見みました。そのあと、食事しょくじをしました。
I watched a movie. After that, I ate.

Cause & Conclusion — だから・そのため・したがって

ExpressionWhen to use itCompare in context
だからCasual, speaker-driven conclusion
疲つかれました。だから、帰かえります。
I am tired. So I am going home.
そのためObjective cause and effect
台風たいふうが近ちかづいています。そのため、試合しあいは中止ちゅうしです。
A typhoon is approaching. For that reason, the match is canceled.
したがってFormal inference from evidence
需要じゅようが増ふえています。したがって、増産ぞうさんが必要ひつようです。
Demand is growing. Therefore, increased production is necessary.

Rephrasing — つまり・すなわち

ExpressionWhen to use itCompare in context
つまりEveryday summary or implication
明日あしたは休やすみです。つまり、学校がっこうはありません。
Tomorrow is a holiday. In other words, there is no school.
すなわちFormal precise identification or definition
成人せいじん、すなわち十八歳以上じゅうはっさいいじょうの人ひとが対象たいしょうです。
Adults, namely people aged 18 and over, are eligible.
Sentence starter vs. clause connector

でも / だけど / ですが / しかし can start a new sentence: 行いきたいです。でも、時間じかんがありません。

けど / ですが can connect clauses inside one sentence: 行いきたいけど、時間じかんがない。 / 行いきたいですが、時間じかんがありません。

だけど also appears within a sentence after a noun or な-adjective (e.g. 便利べんりだけど、高たかい). With an い-adjective or verb, use けど (e.g. 高たかいけど、買かう).

5–4 N5–N4 · Everyday Connectors 14 expressions

そして

Addition

and / and then

朝あさごはんを食たべました。そして、学校がっこうへ行いきました。
I ate breakfast, and then went to school.

Neutral link between statements. It can add information or move a story forward; unlike それから, the time order is not always the main point.

それから

Sequence

after that / then

宿題しゅくだいをしました。それから、テレビを見みました。
I did my homework. After that, I watched TV.

Use when one event follows another in time. For a new item in a list, それと is often more conversational.

でも

Contrast

but / however

この店みせは高たかいです。でも、おいしいです。
This restaurant is expensive, but it is delicious.

The default everyday sentence-opening “but.” It works in casual conversation; in a formal report, しかし is often a better fit.

だから

Result

so / therefore

今日きょうは雨あめです。だから、家いえにいます。
It is raining today, so I am staying home.

Everyday “so”: the second sentence follows from the first. そのため sounds more explanatory; したがって signals a formal logical conclusion.

それに

Addition

besides / moreover

この部屋へやは広ひろいです。それに、明あかるいです。
This room is spacious. Besides, it is bright.

Adds a further reason supporting the same opinion or evaluation, not just another item. Compare それと (one more item).

それと

Addition

and / also / one more thing

パンをください。それと、コーヒーもお願ねがいします。
Bread, please. And a coffee as well.

Often used in conversation to add one more item, question or request. Unlike それに, the new point need not strengthen an argument.

また

Addition

also / in addition

この図書館としょかんは静しずかです。また、無料むりょうで利用りようできます。
This library is quiet. It can also be used for free.

Neutral “also / in addition,” often used in explanations and writing. It can also mean “again” in other contexts; distinguish by context.

それでも

Contrast

even so / nevertheless

雨あめが降ふっていました。それでも、試合しあいは行おこなわれました。
It was raining. Even so, the match took place.

“Even so”: the action or result continues despite an obstacle. ところが instead introduces a surprising development.

ところが

Contrast

however / unexpectedly

早はやく出でました。ところが、電車でんしゃが遅おくれていました。
I left early. However, the train was delayed.

The next event contradicts what was expected. Useful for an unexpected twist in a story; not a general replacement for でも.

そのあと

Sequence

after that

映画えいがを見みました。そのあと、食事しょくじをしました。
We watched a movie. After that, we had a meal.

Explicit time reference (“after that event”). Similar to それから, but more directly points back to a specific time/event.

たとえば

Example

for example

日本にほんの食たべ物ものが好すきです。たとえば、すしやラーメンです。
I like Japanese food, for example sushi and ramen.

Introduces a concrete example, not a second unrelated idea. Often followed by an illustrative noun or sentence.

つまり

Explanation

in other words / in short

明日あしたは休やすみです。つまり、学校がっこうに行いかなくてもいいです。
Tomorrow is a holiday. In other words, I do not have to go to school.

Restates the same point as a summary or conclusion. すなわち is more formal and often defines or identifies something precisely.

だけど

Contrast

but / though

行いきたい。だけど、今日きょうは時間じかんがない。
I want to go. But I have no time today.

Casual spoken transition at the start of a sentence. Also compare 行いきたいけど、時間じかんがない (けど links clauses).

ですが

Contrast

however / but (polite)

行いきたいです。ですが、今日きょうは時間じかんがありません。
I would like to go. However, I have no time today.

Polite and somewhat formal as a sentence opener. Compare 行いきたいですが、時間じかんがありません (within one sentence).

3N3 · Connection Words 6 expressions

しかし

Contrast

however

準備じゅんびは十分じゅうぶんでした。しかし、結果けっかはよくありませんでした。
The preparation was sufficient. However, the result was poor.

Formal or written “however”; appropriate in essays, reports and presentations. でも is more conversational; ところが suggests surprise.

一方いっぽうで

Contrast

on the other hand

都会とかいは便利べんりです。一方いっぽうで、生活費せいかつひが高たかいです。
Cities are convenient. On the other hand, living costs are high.

Shows another side of the same issue or a contrasting perspective, rather than simply denying the previous sentence.

そのため

Result

for that reason

台風たいふうが近ちかづいています。そのため、イベントは中止ちゅうしになりました。
A typhoon is approaching. For that reason, the event was canceled.

Connects a cause to its consequence in a neutral, explanatory tone. Not every contrast or mere sequence is a cause-and-effect relation.

さらに

Addition

furthermore

料金りょうきんが安やすくなりました。さらに、サービスも改善かいぜんされました。
Prices went down. Furthermore, the service improved.

Adds another point and often builds the argument or degree. なお usually supplies a separate supplementary notice.

ただし

Condition

however / provided that

入場にゅうじょうは無料むりょうです。ただし、予約よやくが必要ひつようです。
Admission is free. However, a reservation is required.

Introduces a restriction, exception or condition on what was just said; not simply an opposing opinion.

なお

Addition

additionally / please note

受付うけつけは五時ごじまでです。なお、日曜日にちようびは休業きゅうぎょうです。
Reception is open until five. Please note that it is closed on Sundays.

Adds supplementary practical information, especially in notices or business writing; unlike さらに, it need not strengthen the preceding claim.

2N2 · Connection Words 6 expressions

したがって

Result

therefore / consequently

需要じゅようが増ふえています。したがって、生産量せいさんりょうを増ふやす必要ひつようがあります。
Demand is increasing. Therefore, production needs to increase.

Marks a logical conclusion derived from preceding evidence; formal, common in arguments. Compare casual だから.

それにもかかわらず

Contrast

nevertheless / despite that

何度なんども説明せつめいしました。それにもかかわらず、誤解ごかいは解とけませんでした。
I explained it many times. Nevertheless, the misunderstanding remained.

Strong, formal “despite that,” emphasizing a result contrary to expectation. Less conversational than それでも.

そのうえ

Addition

on top of that

このホテルは駅えきから遠とおいです。そのうえ、料金りょうきんも高たかいです。
This hotel is far from the station. On top of that, it is expensive.

Adds a second fact that compounds the evaluation (positive or negative). Often stronger than neutral また.

もっとも

Qualification

although / that said

この案あんには賛成さんせいです。もっとも、費用ひようには問題もんだいがあります。
I support this proposal. That said, there is an issue with the cost.

After accepting a statement, adds an important reservation or limitation. More qualifying than simple しかし.

すなわち

Explanation

namely / that is

参加さんかできるのは成人せいじん、すなわち十八歳以上じゅうはっさいいじょうの人ひとです。
Those eligible to participate are adults, namely people aged 18 or older.

Formal “namely / that is,” often giving a precise definition or equivalence. つまり is more everyday and can summarize an implication.

あるいは

Choice

or / alternatively

郵送ゆうそう、あるいはメールで提出ていしゅつしてください。
Please submit it by post or, alternatively, by email.

Presents an alternative, often inside the same sentence; not simply an additive “also.”

1N1 · Connection Words 6 expressions

とはいえ

Qualification

having said that / nonetheless

技術ぎじゅつは進歩しんぽしました。とはいえ、課題かだいは残のこっています。
Technology has advanced. That said, challenges remain.

Acknowledges that the previous point is true, then limits what follows from it. Similar to “that said,” not a total rejection.

ひいては

Result

and ultimately / by extension

教育きょういくの充実じゅうじつは、地域ちいきの発展はってん、ひいては社会全体しゃかいぜんたいの成長せいちょうにつながります。
Improving education contributes to regional development and ultimately to society’s growth.

Extends a consequence from a narrower impact to a broader, longer-range one; typically sentence-internal.

それどころか

Contrast

far from that / on the contrary

売上うりあげは減へっていません。それどころか、大幅おおはばに増ふえています。
Sales have not decreased. On the contrary, they have risen substantially.

Corrects or rejects the previous expectation and gives a stronger opposite. Much stronger than ordinary でも.

かくして

Result

thus / in this way

長年ながねんの研究けんきゅうが実みを結むすびました。かくして、新技術しんぎじゅつが誕生たんじょうしたのです。
Years of research bore fruit. Thus, a new technology was born.

Formal or literary narrative transition, “thus / in this way,” after a sequence of developments.

さもなければ

Condition

otherwise

今いますぐ避難ひなんしてください。さもなければ、危険きけんです。
Evacuate immediately. Otherwise, you will be in danger.

Introduces the bad alternative if the preceding instruction/condition is not met; formal “otherwise.”

のみならず

Addition

not only ... but also

この制度せいどは学生がくせいのみならず、社会人しゃかいじんにも役立やくだちます。
This system benefits not only students but also working adults.

Formal “not only A but also B,” often within one sentence; structurally different from sentence-opening また.
