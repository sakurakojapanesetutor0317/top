N3 GRAMMAR · NUANCE & TENDENCY

〜がち・〜っぽい・〜気味

Tendencies, impressions & slight conditions

Choose between a recurring tendency, a characteristic or impression, and a mild current state.

N3Nuance & Tendency3 grammar points
Masu-stemNounAdjective

01 Meaning & Quick Comparison

Compare the meaning and connection before choosing an expression.

〜がち

Tend to… / Be prone to…

Describe an undesirable tendency that happens repeatedly.

雨あめの日ひは家いえにこもりがちです。
On rainy days, I tend to stay home.

〜っぽい

…ish / Like… / Prone to…

Describe an impression, a characteristic, or a tendency.

このスープは少すこし水みずっぽいです。
This soup is a little watery.

〜 気味

A little… / Showing signs of…

Describe a slight condition or trend, often one that is not ideal.

この一週間いっしゅうかん、少すこし寝不足ねぶそく気味ぎみです。
I have been a little short on sleep this week.

02 How to Form

Each expression has its own connection pattern.

〜がち

Verb ます-stem + がち

最近さいきん、朝あさご飯はんを抜ぬきがちです。
Lately I tend to skip breakfast.

Verb ます-stem + がち

忙いそがしいと、約束やくそくを忘わすれがちです。
When busy, I tend to forget appointments.

Noun + がち

彼かれは病気びょうきがちです。
He is often ill.

〜っぽい

Noun + っぽい

その服ふくは子こどもっぽいです。
Those clothes look childish.

Verb ます-stem + っぽい

私わたしは忘わすれっぽいです。
I am forgetful.

Adjective stem + っぽい

この紙かみは安やすっぽいです。
This paper looks cheap.

〜 気味

Verb ます-stem + 気味ぎみ

最近さいきん、疲つかれ気味ぎみです。
I have been a little tired lately.

Noun + 気味ぎみ

今日きょうは風邪かぜ気味ぎみです。
I feel as if I am coming down with a cold.

Verb ます-stem + 気味ぎみ

電車でんしゃが遅おくれ気味ぎみです。
The trains are running a little behind schedule.

04 Usage Notes & Nuance

〜がち · Often negative

〜がち is usually used for unwelcome habits, absences, illness, mistakes, and other negative tendencies.

〜っぽい · Conjugates like an い-adjective

っぽい → っぽくない / っぽかった / っぽくて. It is not a な-adjective.

〜 気味 · A slight degree

気味ぎみ usually suggests “somewhat” or “showing signs of,” not a serious or extreme state.

05 Choose the Right Expression

These expressions are related, but they are not interchangeable.

〜がち

Describe an undesirable tendency that happens repeatedly.

最近さいきん、忘わすれがちです。
Lately I tend to forget things.

〜っぽい

Describe an impression, a characteristic, or a tendency.

この服ふくは安やすっぽいです。
These clothes look cheap.

〜 気味

Describe a slight condition or trend, often one that is not ideal.

最近さいきん、疲つかれ気味ぎみです。
I feel slightly tired lately.

06 Common Mistakes

〜がち · Mistake 1

✕ 忘わすれますがちです。
✓ 忘わすれがちです。
Use the ます-stem, not ます.

〜っぽい · Mistake 1

✕ 忘わすれますっぽいです。
✓ 忘わすれっぽいです。
Use the ます-stem before っぽい.

〜 気味 · Mistake 1

✕ 疲つかれます気味ぎみです。
✓ 疲つかれ気味ぎみです。
Use the ます-stem before 気味ぎみ.
