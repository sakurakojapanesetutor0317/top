N3 GRAMMAR · REMEMBERING / CHECKING

〜っけ

…again? · Was it…?

Ask yourself or someone else to confirm a detail you once knew but cannot quite remember.

N3Memory & ConfirmationPlain form / だ / た + っけ
Plain FormDictionary FormNai-formTa-formAdjectiveNoun

01 Meaning — …Again? / Was It…?

〜っけ is used when you are trying to remember something or checking a detail you think you knew before. It often sounds like “What was it again?” or “Was it…?”

会議かいぎは何時なんじからだっけ？
What time does the meeting start again?
あの人ひとの名前なまえ、何なんだったっけ？
What was that person’s name again?
Conversation tone

っけ is common in casual speech. It can be said to yourself or used to check with someone else.

02 How to Form — Remembering a Detail

Common forms include 〜だっけ / 〜だったっけ after nouns and な-adjectives, and 〜たっけ / 〜るんだっけ with verbs. Polite forms such as 〜でしたっけ / 〜ましたっけ are also used.

Noun / な-adjective + だっけ / だったっけ
Verb past form + っけ
Verb plain form + んだ + っけ

Noun

今日きょうは何曜日なんようびだっけ？
What day is it again?

Past event

この映画えいが、もう見みたっけ？
Have I seen this movie already?

Plan / fact

彼かれも来くるんだっけ？
Was he coming too?

Polite

会議かいぎは何時なんじからでしたっけ？
What time was the meeting again? (polite)

03 Everyday Examples — Checking What You Remember

次つぎの授業じゅぎょうは金曜日きんようびだっけ？
Is the next lesson on Friday again?
あのカフェ、駅えきの近ちかくだったっけ？
Was that café near the station?
昨日きのう、彼かれに連絡れんらくしたっけ？
Did I contact him yesterday?
宿題しゅくだいは何なにをすればいいんだっけ？
What was I supposed to do for homework again?

04 Nouns, Verbs & Polite Forms

Noun + だっけ

明日あしたは休やすみだっけ？
Is tomorrow a day off again?

Verb + たっけ

予約よやくしたっけ？
Did I make a reservation?

Verb + んだっけ

彼女かのじょも行いくんだっけ？
Was she going too?

Polite + でしたっけ

お名前なまえは何なんでしたっけ？
What was your name again? (polite; may sound forgetful)
Social nuance

Asking someone’s name again with っけ may reveal that you forgot it. Use extra care with people you have just met or in formal situations.

05 Compare — っけ / かな / でしょうか

っけ — recalling something known

会議かいぎは3時じからだっけ？
Was the meeting at three again?

かな — wondering

会議かいぎは3時じからかな？
I wonder if the meeting is at three.

でしょうか — polite inquiry

会議かいぎは3時じからでしょうか。
Does the meeting start at three? (polite)
Remembering vs. wondering

っけ implies you are trying to retrieve or verify a memory. かな expresses wondering without necessarily implying prior knowledge.

06 Common Mistakes

Use だっけ after a noun

✕ 今日きょうは何曜日なんようびたっけ？
✓ 今日きょうは何曜日なんようびだっけ？
Use だっけ / だったっけ with nouns; in casual speech だ may sometimes be omitted.

Past verb + っけ

✕ 予約よやくするっけ？
✓ 予約よやくしたっけ？
To check whether you already did something, use the past form.

Avoid overusing it in formal requests

△ お名前なまえは何なんでしたっけ？
✓ お名前なまえをもう一度いちど教おしえていただけますか。
In formal contexts, a more considerate re-question can be preferable.
