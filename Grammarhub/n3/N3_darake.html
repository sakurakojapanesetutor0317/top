N3 GRAMMAR · EXCESS & QUANTITY

〜だらけ

Covered in… / Full of…

Say that something is full of an unwanted substance, mistake, or problem.

N3Excess & QuantityNoun + だらけ
Noun

01 Meaning & Core Use

Use 〜だらけ when there is a lot of something, usually undesirable, spread over a place or object or present throughout something. It often suggests “far too much.”

子こどもが遊あそんだあと、床ゆかはおもちゃだらけでした。
After the children played, the floor was covered with toys.
この書類しょるいは間違まちがいだらけです。
This document is full of mistakes.

02 How to Form

Choose the connection that matches the part of speech or verb form.

Noun + だらけ

靴くつが泥どろだらけです。
My shoes are covered in mud.

Noun + だらけ + の + noun

間違まちがいだらけの作文さくぶんです。
It is an essay full of mistakes.

Noun + だらけ + に + なる

服ふくが砂すなだらけになりました。
My clothes became covered in sand.

03 Everyday Examples

Notice how the meaning changes with the situation.

子こどもが遊あそんだあと、床ゆかはおもちゃだらけでした。
After the children played, the floor was covered with toys.
この書類しょるいは間違まちがいだらけです。
This document is full of mistakes.
庭にわで転ころんで、ズボンが泥どろだらけになりました。
I fell in the garden, and my trousers got covered in mud.
古ふるい本ほんがほこりだらけになっています。
The old books are covered in dust.

04 Usage Notes & Nuance

Usually an unwanted amount

Typical nouns include 泥どろ (mud), ほこり (dust), 間違まちがい (mistakes), 傷きず (scratches), and ゴミ (rubbish).

Different from いっぱい

いっぱい simply means full or many; だらけ usually carries a negative evaluation or the image of something being covered.

05 Compare Similar Expressions

The expressions below are not always interchangeable.

だらけ — negative

部屋へやがゴミだらけです。
The room is full of rubbish.

いっぱい — neutral

部屋へやに人ひとがいっぱいいます。
There are lots of people in the room.

まみれ — physically smeared

手てが油あぶらまみれです。
My hands are smeared with oil.

06 Common Mistakes

Mistake 1

✕ 泥どろのだらけです。
✓ 泥どろだらけです。
Attach だらけ directly to a noun.

Mistake 2

✕ 間違まちがいだらけな作文さくぶんです。
✓ 間違まちがいだらけの作文さくぶんです。
Use だらけの before a noun.

Mistake 3

✕ 服ふくが泥どろだらけをなりました。
✓ 服ふくが泥どろだらけになりました。
Use 〜だらけになる for a change of state.
