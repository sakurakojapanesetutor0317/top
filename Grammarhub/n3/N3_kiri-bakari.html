N3 GRAMMAR · LIMITATION & REPETITION

〜きり・〜っきり・〜ばかり

Only once / Nothing but…

Distinguish a single event followed by no further change from repeatedly doing or choosing the same thing.

N3Limitation & Repetition2 grammar points
Ta-formNounNumberTe-form

01 Meaning & Quick Comparison

Compare the meaning and connection before choosing an expression.

〜きり／〜っきり

Only… / Ever since… (and not again)

Express being alone or doing something just once, or a state continuing since an event.

今日きょうは一人ひとりきりで過すごしたいです。
I want to spend today entirely on my own.

〜ばかり

Nothing but… / Keep doing…

Describe someone doing or choosing mostly the same thing, often too much.

休やすみの間あいだ、ゲームばかりしていました。
I did nothing but play games during the break.

02 How to Form

Each expression has its own connection pattern.

〜きり／〜っきり

Quantity / person + きり

今日きょうは一人ひとりきりで過すごしたいです。
I want to spend today completely alone.

Number + きり

彼かれに会あったのは一度いちどきりです。
I have met him only once.

Verb た + きり

友達ともだちに会あったきり、連絡れんらくしていません。
I have not contacted my friend since we met.

〜ばかり

Noun + ばかり

甘あまいものばかり食たべています。
I keep eating mostly sweets.

Verb て + ばかりいる

弟おとうとは遊あそんでばかりいます。
My younger brother does nothing but play.

Noun + ばかり + verb

彼かれは文句もんくばかり言いいます。
He does nothing but complain.

04 Usage Notes & Nuance

〜きり／〜っきり · Quantity and exclusivity

一人ひとりきり and 一度いちどきり emphasize “only one person” or “only one time.” They often sound stronger than だけ.

〜きり／〜っきり · Past action with no continuation

〜たきり often suggests that the person has not returned, met again, or contacted someone since the past event. The following clause makes that lack of continuation clear.

〜ばかり · Not necessarily literally every time

ばかり is often an exaggeration for “mostly / too much.” It does not always mean that absolutely nothing else happens.

〜ばかり · Do not confuse てばかり with たばかり

食たべてばかりいる means “keep eating”; 食たべたばかり means “have just eaten.” They are different patterns.

05 Choose the Right Expression

These expressions are related, but they are not interchangeable.

〜きり／〜っきり

Express being alone or doing something just once, or a state continuing since an event.

今日きょうは一人ひとりきりです。
I am completely alone today.

〜ばかり

Describe someone doing or choosing mostly the same thing, often too much.

ゲームばかりしています。
I do little besides playing games.

06 Common Mistakes

〜きり／〜っきり · Mistake 1

✕ 一人ひとりのきりで行いきます。
✓ 一人ひとりきりで行いきます。
Attach きり directly to the number or person expression.

〜きり／〜っきり · Mistake 2

✕ 会あうきり、連絡れんらくしていません。
✓ 会あったきり、連絡れんらくしていません。
Use the た-form for “ever since.”

〜ばかり · Mistake 1

✕ ゲームをばかりしています。
✓ ゲームばかりしています。
Put ばかり directly after the noun here.

〜ばかり · Mistake 2

✕ 遊あそぶばかりいます。
✓ 遊あそんでばかりいます。
Use the て-form + ばかりいる.
