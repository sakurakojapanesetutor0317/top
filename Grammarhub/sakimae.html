先さき・前まえ・後あと

Saki · Mae · Ato / Go / Ushiro

Understand how Japanese describes what comes first, what comes before or after, and what lies ahead or behind — in time, order, and space.

N5–N3 expressionsTime & sequenceEnglish explanations

01 The Key Idea: Choose a Reference Point

English words such as before, after, ahead, previous, next, first and behind do not match Japanese words one-to-one. The kanji also have multiple readings: 先（さき／せん）, 前（まえ／ぜん）, and 後（あと／ご／うしろ）. Meaning and pronunciation depend on time, order, space, and the complete expression.

Do not memorize “先さき = future” and “前まえ = past.”

For example, 先さきほど means “a little while ago,” while 寝ねる前まえに can describe something that will happen tomorrow. The important question is: Before or after what? First in which sequence? Ahead of which position?

02 先さき（さき） — First, Ahead, or Further Along

Think of 先さき as a point earlier in an order or further along a direction or sequence. It can point toward the future, but not always.

先さきに 食たべてください。

Please eat first. (Order: before someone else.)

お先さきにどうぞ。

After you. / You go first.

この先さきに 駅えきがあります。

There is a station further ahead.

先さきほど 電話でんわしました。

I called a little while ago. (Fixed expression referring to the recent past.)

先さきのことは まだ わかりません。

I don't know what lies ahead yet.

Important: 先さきに is not the same as 〜前まえに.

先さきに highlights who or what goes first. 〜前まえに says that one action happens before a reference action or time.

前まえ（まえ） — Before a Point / In Front Of

前まえ expresses a position before a reference point. That reference point may be in the past, present, or future. In space, 前まえ means in front of.

Verb (dictionary form) + 前まえに / Noun + の前まえに / Time period + 前まえに

寝ねる前まえに 歯はを 磨みがきます。

I brush my teeth before going to bed.

明日あした、寝ねる前まえに 電話でんわします。

I'll call tomorrow before going to bed. (Both events are in the future.)

三年さんねん前まえに 日本にほんに 来きました。

I came to Japan three years ago.

駅えきの前まえに バス停ていがあります。

There is a bus stop in front of the station.

前まえの仕事しごとは 大変たいへんでした。

My previous job was demanding.

〜前まえに is not necessarily “ago.”

三年前さんねんまえ means “three years ago,” but 食たべる前まえに means “before eating,” and 会議かいぎの前まえに means “before the meeting.” The construction changes the meaning.

04 前まえ（ぜん） — Before / Previous in Compound Words

The same kanji 前まえ also has the reading ぜん. This reading is common in compound words (熟語じゅくご), often made from kanji. Both まえ and ぜん can point to something earlier, but they are not freely interchangeable. Learn the pronunciation as part of each word.

午前ごぜん 九時くじ

9 a.m. — 午前ごぜん is the fixed word for a.m. / before noon.

以前いぜん、東京とうきょうに 住すんでいました。

I used to live in Tokyo. — 以前いぜん means previously / at an earlier time.

旅行りょこうの 前日ぜんじつに 準備じゅんびしました。

I prepared the day before the trip. — 前日ぜんじつ is the preceding day relative to an event.

事前じぜんに 予約よやくしてください。

Please make a reservation in advance. — 事前じぜんに emphasizes preparation before an event.

前回ぜんかいの 授業じゅぎょうを 復習ふくしゅうします。

I'll review the previous lesson. — 前回ぜんかい means the previous occurrence.

前後ぜんごの 順番じゅんばんを 確認かくにんします。

I'll check the order of what comes before and after. — 前後ぜんご combines ぜん and ご.

Compare the readings

〜の前まえに（まえに） = before a specific event; 事前じぜんに（じぜんに） = in advance, beforehand; 以前いぜん（いぜん） = previously / before a given time; 午前ごぜん（ごぜん） = a.m. These overlap in English, but their grammar and usage differ.

Similarly, 先さき can be read さき or せん (先週せんしゅう・先月せんげつ), while 後あと has several readings. The kanji alone does not determine the pronunciation.

05 後あと（あと） — After an Event / What Comes Later

後あと（あと） is used when something happens after a reference event or when referring to what remains. It is especially common in everyday conversation.

Verb (た-form) + 後あとで / Noun + の後あとで / 後あとで = later

ご飯はんを 食たべた後あとで、勉強べんきょうします。

I'll study after eating.

授業じゅぎょうの後あとで 話はなしましょう。

Let's talk after class.

後あとで 連絡れんらくします。

I'll contact you later.

あと五分ごふんです。

Five minutes left / five more minutes.

Grammar contrast

食たべる前まえに (dictionary form + before) versus 食たべた後あとで (た-form + after). The た-form here marks a completed action relative to the next action; it does not mean that the whole sentence must be about the past.

06 後あと（ご） — After, in Compound Time Words

The reading ご appears in many set compound words, especially formal or compact time expressions. You generally cannot freely replace あと with ご.

食後しょくごに 薬くすりを 飲のみます。

I take medicine after meals.

授業後じゅぎょうごに 会あいましょう。

Let's meet after class.

午後ごご三時さんじです。

It's 3 p.m. (午後ごご is a fixed word meaning p.m./afternoon.)

一週間後いっしゅうかんごに 帰かえります。

I'll return one week later / in one week.

あと vs. ご

授業じゅぎょうの後あとで (じゅぎょうのあとで) is conversational; 授業じゅぎょう後ご (じゅぎょうご) is a compact compound. 一週間後いっしゅうかんご is pronounced いっしゅうかんご, while あと一週間いっしゅうかん means “one more week / one week remaining.” These are different constructions.

07 後うしろ（うしろ） — Behind, in Space

後うしろ usually describes physical position, not a later point in time. It contrasts naturally with 前まえ meaning “in front of.”

学校がっこうの後うしろに 公園こうえんがあります。

There is a park behind the school.

私わたしの後うしろに 並ならんでください。

Please line up behind me.

Compare:

駅えきの前まえ = in front of the station; 駅えきの後うしろ = behind the station; 駅えきに着ついた後あと = after arriving at the station. The last one is about time, not position.

“Beforehand” in English

For “prepare beforehand,” Japanese often uses 事前じぜんに（じぜんに）, あらかじめ, or 前まえもって（まえもって）. 〜前まえに simply means “before [a specified event]”; it does not always carry the idea of preparation.

09 Common Expressions & Idioms

Some expressions are best learned as complete phrases. Their meanings cannot always be predicted from a single translation of 先さき, 前まえ, or 後あと.

後先あとさきを 考かんがえずに 行動こうどうしました。

I acted without thinking about the consequences. Literally, “without thinking about what comes after and before.”

先週せんしゅう、先月せんげつ、先日せんじつ

Last week, last month, the other day. In these fixed compounds, 先さき is read せん and points to an earlier period.

前日ぜんじつ ／ 翌日よくじつ

The previous day / the following day (relative to another event).

今後こんご ／ 以後いご ／ 最後さいご

From now on / after that point / the end or last. 後あと is read ご in these compounds.

Why can 先さき mean “earlier”?

Because “first in a sequence” and “further ahead along a path” are two different ways to organize events. In 先週せんしゅう, the week comes earlier than this week; in この先さき, the point lies further ahead. This is why a single English gloss cannot cover all uses.

10 Check Your Understanding

Try choosing the correct expression before opening each answer.

1. “I'll call before I go to bed.” — 寝ねる（先さきに／前まえに／後あとで）電話でんわします。

前まえに — 寝ねる前まえに 電話でんわします。 “Before going to bed.”

2. “Please go first.” — （先さきに／前まえに／後あとで）行いってください。

先さきに — 先さきに 行いってください。 Order: go before the other person.

3. “Let's talk after class.” — 授業じゅぎょう（の前まえに／の後あとで／の先さきに）話はなしましょう。

の後あと — 授業じゅぎょうの後あとで 話はなしましょう。

4. “There is a park behind the school.” — 学校がっこうの（前まえで／後うしろ／先さき）に公園こうえんがあります。

後うしろ — 学校がっこうの後うしろに 公園こうえんがあります。
