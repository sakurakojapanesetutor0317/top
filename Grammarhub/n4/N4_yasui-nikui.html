N4 GRAMMAR · EASE & DIFFICULTY

〜やすい・〜にくい

Easy to do / Difficult to do / Prone to happen

Learn how to form 〜やすい and 〜にくい with the verb ます-stem. Compare ease, difficulty, and tendencies, then practice their adjective forms in everyday Japanese.

N4Ease & DifficultyVerb ます-stem + やすい / にくい
Masu-stem

01 〜やすい・〜にくい — Easy or Difficult to Do

Attach 〜やすい or 〜にくい to a verb to describe how easy or difficult an action is. They can also express a tendency: something happens easily or is unlikely to happen.

〜やすい — easy to do

この本ほんは読よみやすいです。
This book is easy to read.

〜にくい — difficult to do

この字じは読よみにくいです。
This character is difficult to read.

〜やすい — happens easily

このコップは割われやすいです。
This cup breaks easily.

〜にくい — does not happen easily

このコップは割われにくいです。
This cup does not break easily.
Two useful meanings

Ease or difficulty: 使つかいやすい = easy to use. Tendency: 忘わすれやすい = easy to forget / tend to forget. 〜やすい does not always mean an action happens frequently; it describes how readily it can happen.

02 How to Form Them — Remove ます First

Start with the ます-form of a verb. Remove ます, then add やすい or にくい. The part left after removing ます is often called the ます-stem. Do not attach the endings to the dictionary form.

Verb ます-form − ます + やすい／にくい
書かきます → 書かき → 書かきやすい／にくい
読よみます → 読よみ → 読よみやすい／にくい
食たべます → 食たべ → 食たべやすい／にくい
します → し → しやすい／にくい
来きます → 来き → 来きやすい／にくい
話はなします → 話はなしやすい
Easy to talk / easy to speak.
見みます → 見みにくい
Difficult to see.
覚おぼえます → 覚おぼえやすい
Easy to remember.
来きます → 来きにくい
Difficult to come (to a place).
Remember the irregular verbs

する → しやすい／しにくい. 来くる → 来きやすい／来きにくい. The reading of 来くる changes to き in the ます-stem.

03 〜やすい — Easy to Use, Read, See, or Do

Use 〜やすい when a tool, object, explanation, or situation makes an action easy to do. This is common in everyday descriptions and product reviews.

このペンは書かきやすいです。
This pen is easy to write with.
この説明せつめいは分わかりやすいです。
This explanation is easy to understand.
この靴くつは歩あるきやすいです。
These shoes are comfortable to walk in.
このアプリは使つかいやすいです。
This app is easy to use.
大おおきい字じは見みやすいです。
Large letters are easy to see.
この単語たんごは覚おぼえやすいです。
This word is easy to remember.
The subject is often a thing

In “このペンは書かきやすい,” the pen is not writing by itself. The sentence means the pen makes writing easy for the user.

04 〜にくい — Difficult to Use, Read, See, or Do

Use 〜にくい when something makes an action hard or inconvenient. It describes difficulty, not necessarily impossibility.

この字じは小ちいさくて読よみにくいです。
These letters are small and difficult to read.
この道みちは歩あるきにくいです。
This road is difficult to walk on.
この瓶びんは開あけにくいです。
This bottle is hard to open.
音おとが小ちいさくて聞ききにくいです。
The sound is quiet and difficult to hear.
この名前なまえは覚おぼえにくいです。
This name is difficult to remember.
この質問しつもんは答こたえにくいです。
This question is difficult to answer.
Difficult does not mean impossible

開あけにくい = hard to open. 開あけられない = cannot open. The first suggests difficulty; the second says the action cannot be done.

05 〜やすい・〜にくい — Tendencies and Likelihood

These endings also describe how readily something happens. The verb does not need to be an intentional action. This use is important with verbs such as 割われる (break), 滑すべる (slip), and 忘わすれる (forget).

Happens easily

この道みちは雨あめの日ひに滑すべりやすいです。
This road is slippery on rainy days.

Does not happen easily

この靴くつは滑すべりにくいです。
These shoes do not slip easily.

Prone to something

私わたしは風邪かぜをひきやすいです。
I tend to catch colds easily.

Resistant to something

この服ふくは汚よごれにくいです。
These clothes do not get dirty easily.
Not the same as “often”

風邪かぜをひきやすい describes a tendency or susceptibility. 風邪かぜをよくひく says that someone actually catches colds often. The two ideas can overlap, but they are not identical.

06 Same Verb, Opposite Meanings

Try changing only the ending. The verb stays in the ます-stem, while the ending changes the meaning.

Easy to read

この本ほんは読よみやすいです。
This book is easy to read.

Difficult to read

この本ほんは読よみにくいです。
This book is difficult to read.

Easy to open

このドアは開あけやすいです。
This door is easy to open.

Difficult to open

このドアは開あけにくいです。
This door is hard to open.

Breaks easily

このグラスは割われやすいです。
This glass breaks easily.

Does not break easily

このグラスは割われにくいです。
This glass does not break easily.
Important nuance

割われにくい does not mean “cannot break.” It means breaking is less likely or less easy. Likewise, 読よみにくい does not mean “cannot read.”

07 Conjugation — Treat Them Like い-Adjectives

After you attach 〜やすい or 〜にくい, the result behaves like an い-adjective. Change the final い to make negatives, past forms, and connections.

Present: 読よみやすいです / 読よみにくいです
Negative: 読よみやすくないです / 読よみにくくないです
Past: 読よみやすかったです / 読よみにくかったです
Past negative: 読よみやすくなかったです / 読よみにくくなかったです
この本ほんは読よみやすかったです。
This book was easy to read.
昨日きのうの説明せつめいは分わかりにくかったです。
Yesterday’s explanation was hard to understand.
このペンは書かきやすくないです。
This pen is not easy to write with.
このアプリは使つかいやすくて、便利べんりです。
This app is easy to use and convenient.
N5 · い-Adjective Conjugation
Negative is not automatically the opposite

読よみやすくない means “not easy to read.” 読よみにくい more directly means “difficult to read.” These often overlap, but they do not always have exactly the same strength.

08 〜やすくなる・〜にくくなる — Changes Over Time

Because these expressions behave like い-adjectives, use 〜くなる to describe a change: become easier or harder to do. Use 〜くする when someone deliberately makes something easier or harder.

Become easier — 〜やすくなる

練習れんしゅうして、日本語にほんごが話はなしやすくなりました。
After practicing, Japanese became easier to speak.

Become harder — 〜にくくなる

暗くらくなると、字じが見みにくくなります。
When it gets dark, letters become harder to see.

Make easier — 〜やすくする

字じを大おおきくして、読よみやすくしました。
I made the text larger to make it easier to read.

Make harder — 〜にくくする

パスワードを長ながくして、覚おぼえにくくしました。
I made the password longer, making it harder to remember.
Connection to 〜ようになる

話はなせるようになる focuses on gaining the ability to speak. 話はなしやすくなる focuses on speaking becoming easier. These are related but different meanings.

09 〜やすい vs. Easy / 〜にくい vs. Difficult

The words 易やさしい (easy), 簡単かんたん (simple/easy), and 難むずかしい (difficult) describe something more generally. Verb + 〜やすい／にくい tells you which action is easy or difficult.

General description

この本ほんは易やさしいです。
This book is easy (for example, its content is simple).

Specific action

この本ほんは読よみやすいです。
This book is easy to read (for example, the wording or layout).

General description

この問題もんだいは難むずかしいです。
This problem is difficult.

Specific action

この質問しつもんは答こたえにくいです。
This question is difficult to answer.
Do not confuse やすい with 安やすい

安やすい is an independent い-adjective meaning cheap / inexpensive. 読よみやすい means easy to read. The sound やすい is the same, but the structure and meaning are different.

10 〜にくい vs. Potential Form — Difficult or Impossible?

〜にくい means an action is hard to do. A negative potential form often says the action cannot be done. Use the right expression for the situation.

Difficult to open

この箱はこは開あけにくいです。
This box is hard to open.

Cannot open

この箱はこは開あけられません。
I cannot open this box.

Difficult to eat

この魚さかなは骨ほねが多おおくて食たべにくいです。
This fish is difficult to eat because it has many bones.

Cannot eat

アレルギーがあるので、この魚さかなは食たべられません。
I cannot eat this fish because I have an allergy.
N5 · Potential Form
Use context

In everyday conversation, “できない” can sometimes express practical difficulty, not absolute impossibility. For N4 study, remember the basic distinction: 〜にくい = hard; negative potential = cannot.

11 Extra Expression — 〜づらい

You may also hear 〜づらい (Verb ます-stem + づらい). It is similar to 〜にくい, but it can highlight personal discomfort, hesitation, or a subjective feeling of difficulty. This is an extra expression to recognize, not a form you need to master before 〜にくい.

〜にくい — difficult to say

これは言いいにくいです。
This is hard to say.

〜づらい — awkward to say

これは言いいづらいです。
This is difficult/awkward for me to say.

〜にくい — hard to walk

この道みちは歩あるきにくいです。
This road is difficult to walk on.

〜づらい — uncomfortable to walk

この道みちは歩あるきづらいです。
This road feels difficult to walk on.
Not an absolute rule

〜にくい and 〜づらい often overlap. The personal/psychological nuance is a useful tendency, but both can describe physical or practical difficulty depending on the context.

12 Everyday Conversations

These patterns are useful when comparing products, asking for feedback, or talking about difficulties in daily life.

A：このペンはどうですか。
B：とても書かきやすいですよ。
A: How is this pen? B: It is very easy to write with.
A：この説明せつめい、分わかりますか。
B：すみません。少すこし分わかりにくいです。
A: Do you understand this explanation? B: Sorry, it is a little difficult to understand.
A：この靴くつはどうですか。
B：軽かるくて歩あるきやすいです。
A: How are these shoes? B: They are light and easy to walk in.
A：このコップは割われやすいですか。
B：いいえ。割われにくいですよ。
A: Does this cup break easily? B: No, it is not easy to break.

13 Common Mistakes

Remove ます first

✕ 読よみますやすいです。
✓ 読よみやすいです。
Remove ます, then attach やすい.

Do not attach to dictionary form

✕ 食たべるにくいです。
✓ 食たべにくいです。
Use the ます-stem: 食たべます → 食たべ.

Conjugate like an い-adjective

✕ 読よみやすいじゃないです。
✓ 読よみやすくないです。
The standard negative form is 〜やすくない.

Difficult is not impossible

開あけにくいです。
開あけられません。
Hard to open / cannot open. Choose the intended meaning.
Check both the form and the meaning

First make the ます-stem. Then decide whether the sentence describes ease, difficulty, or a tendency. Finally, conjugate the ending as an い-adjective if necessary.
