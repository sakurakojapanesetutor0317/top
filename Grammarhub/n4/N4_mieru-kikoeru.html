見みえます・聞きこえます

Something is visible / audible naturally

Describe what you can see or hear without deliberately trying. Learn why these expressions usually take が, and how they differ from the potential forms 見みられます and 聞きけます.

N4Lesson 27Natural perception · が

01 What Do 見みえます and 聞きこえます Mean?

見みえます means “is visible / can be seen,” and 聞きこえます means “is audible / can be heard.” They describe what reaches your eyes or ears naturally, not something you choose or make an effort to see or hear.

What is visible / audible + が + 見みえます / 聞きこえます
ここから富士山ふじさんが見みえます。
You can see Mt. Fuji from here.
外そとから鳥とりの声こえが聞きこえます。
I can hear birds chirping outside. (Literally, birds' voices are audible.)
あそこに海うみが見みえます。
You can see the sea over there.
Key idea

These are not the regular potential forms of 見みます and 聞ききます. Their meaning focuses on natural visibility and audibility.

02 Particles — Especially が

Use が to mark the thing you can see or hear. The place you look from can be marked by から.

Place + から + Thing + が + 見みえます
Sound + が + 聞きこえます
この部屋へやから公園こうえんが見みえます。
You can see the park from this room.
隣となりの部屋へやから音楽おんがくが聞きこえます。
I can hear music coming from the room next door.
窓まどから何なにが見みえますか。
What can you see from the window?
が vs. を

山やまが見みえます = The mountain is visible.
山やまを見みます = I look at the mountain (an intentional action).

03 見みえます vs. 見みられます / 聞きこえます vs. 聞きけます

Both pairs can translate as “can see / can hear,” but the focus is different. 見みえます・聞こえます describe what is naturally perceptible. 見みられます・聞けます describe what is possible or available to see or listen to.

見みえます — naturally visible

窓まどから星ほしが見みえます。
The stars are visible from the window.

見みられます — possible to see

この美術館びじゅつかんでは有名ゆうめいな絵えが見みられます。
You can see famous paintings at this museum.

聞こえます — naturally audible

外そとから音楽おんがくが聞きこえます。
I can hear music coming from outside.

聞けます — possible to listen to

このアプリで日本語にほんごのラジオが聞きけます。
You can listen to Japanese radio on this app.
Useful distinction

Use 聞こえます for a sound reaching your ears, even when you are not trying to listen. Use 聞きけます when there is an opportunity or ability to listen.

04 Negative and Past Forms

見みえます and 聞きこえます conjugate like regular Group 2 verbs in polite speech. There is no need to change them into potential forms.

見みえます

見みえます
見みえません
見みえました
見みえませんでした
is visible / isn't visible / was visible / wasn't visible

聞こえます

聞きこえます
聞きこえません
聞きこえました
聞きこえませんでした
is audible / isn't audible / was audible / wasn't audible
暗くらくて、何なにも見みえません。
It's dark, so I can't see anything.
さっきは声こえが聞きこえませんでした。
I couldn't hear your voice earlier.

05 Everyday Situations

These expressions are especially useful when talking about distance, noise, darkness, and visibility.

眼鏡めがねをかけないと、字じがよく見みえません。
If I don't wear glasses, I can't see the letters clearly.
後うしろの席せきから黒板こくばんが見みえますか。
Can you see the blackboard from the back seat?
周まわりがうるさくて、先生せんせいの声こえが聞きこえません。
It's noisy around me, so I can't hear the teacher's voice.
もう少すこし大おおきな声こえで話はなしてください。よく聞きこえません。
Please speak a little louder. I can't hear you well.

06 Common Mistakes

Use が for the visible thing

✕ 富士山ふじさんを見みえます。
✓ 富士山ふじさんが見みえます。
見みえます normally takes が, not を.

Don't mix up perception and possibility

遠とおくに海うみが見みえます。
ここでは映画えいがが見みられます。
The sea is visible in the distance. / You can watch movies here.

見みます is intentional

空そらを見みます。
空そらに星ほしが見みえます。
I look at the sky. / I can see stars in the sky.

聞ききます is intentional

音楽おんがくを聞ききます。
音楽おんがくが聞きこえます。
I listen to music. / Music is audible.
