N4 GRAMMAR · SIMULTANEOUS ACTIONS

〜ながら While Doing …

While doing A, do B / Do two things at the same time

Use 〜ながら to describe two actions performed at the same time by the same person. Learn the ます-stem connection and how it differs from 〜てから and 〜間あいだに.

N4Simultaneous ActionsVerb ます-stem + ながら

01 What Does 〜ながら Mean?

〜ながら connects two actions that happen at the same time. In a typical sentence, the same person performs both actions.

Action A (ます-stem) + ながら + Action B
音楽おんがくを聞ききながら、勉強べんきょうします。
I study while listening to music.
朝あさごはんを食たべながら、ニュースを見みます。
I watch the news while eating breakfast.
歩あるきながら、友達ともだちと話はなしました。
I talked with my friend while walking.
Two actions, one person

In 「音楽おんがくを聞ききながら、勉強べんきょうします」, the same person listens to music and studies. The action after ながら is usually the main focus.

02 How to Make It — Start with ます

Remove ます from the polite form and add ながら. This rule works for all verb groups; you do not need the て-form.

ます-form − ます + ながら

Group 1

聞ききます → 聞ききながら
while listening

Group 1

読よみます → 読よみながら
while reading

Group 2

食たべます → 食たべながら
while eating

Group 2

見みます → 見みながら
while watching

Group 3

します → しながら
while doing

Group 3

勉強べんきょうします → 勉強べんきょうしながら
while studying
Common mistake

✕ 食たべてながら → ✓ 食たべながら
✕ 聞きくながら → ✓ 聞ききながら

03 The Main Action Comes After ながら

The action after ながら is usually what the speaker wants to emphasize. Changing the order can change the focus, even when the two actions happen together.

Main action: studying

音楽おんがくを聞ききながら、勉強べんきょうします。
I study while listening to music.

Main action: listening

勉強べんきょうしながら、音楽おんがくを聞ききます。
I listen to music while studying.
Not a strict rule

The last action is typically the main one, but context can affect emphasis. Both actions overlap in time.

04 Same Subject — Who Is Doing the Actions?

For the basic simultaneous-action meaning, both actions normally have the same subject. If two different people are doing different things, 〜間あいだに is often more appropriate.

✓ Same person — ながら

私わたしは料理りょうりしながら、音楽おんがくを聞ききます。
I listen to music while I cook. (I do both.)

Different people — 間あいだに

母ははが料理りょうりしている間あいだに、私わたしは宿題しゅくだいをします。
While my mother is cooking, I do my homework. (Two people.)
Why this matters

Avoid using the basic 〜ながら pattern to combine two actions with different subjects. Use 〜間あいだに or another time expression instead.

05 Tense Goes at the End

ながら does not change for past, present, or future. The verb after ながら carries the sentence tense.

音楽おんがくを聞ききながら、勉強べんきょうします。
I study while listening to music.
音楽おんがくを聞ききながら、勉強べんきょうしました。
I studied while listening to music.
今夜こんやはテレビを見みながら、ごはんを食たべます。
Tonight, I’ll eat while watching TV.
One ending controls the tense

Do not try to put a past-tense ending on the ながら verb: ✕ 聞ききましたながら. Use 聞ききながら and put the past ending on the final verb.

06 〜ながら vs. 〜てから vs. 〜間あいだに

All three expressions can describe events around the same time, but they have different meanings.

〜ながら — at the same time

テレビを見みながら、ごはんを食たべます。
I eat while watching TV. (overlapping actions)

〜てから — first A, then B

テレビを見みてから、ごはんを食たべます。
I eat after watching TV. (sequential actions)

〜間あいだに — during a time period

子こどもが寝ねている間あいだに、掃除そうじします。
I clean while my child is asleep. (an event during that time)
Quick check

ながら = both actions overlap. てから = the first action finishes before the next. 間あいだに = something happens during a period, and the subjects may be different.

07 Everyday Examples

Use 〜ながら to talk about routines, hobbies, and things you do together.

電車でんしゃに乗のりながら、本ほんを読よみます。
I read a book while riding the train.
友達ともだちと話はなしながら、公園こうえんを歩あるきました。
I walked through the park while talking with a friend.
動画どうがを見みながら、日本語にほんごを勉強べんきょうしています。
I’m studying Japanese while watching videos.
運転うんてんしながら、スマホを見みないでください。
Please do not look at your phone while driving.
笑わらいながら、話はなしました。
We talked while laughing.

08 Common Mistakes

Use the ます-stem

✕ 読よむながら
✓ 読よみながら
Remove ます first; do not use the dictionary form.

Do not add て

✕ 食たべてながら
✓ 食たべながら
〜ながら connects directly to the ます-stem.

Do not mix up simultaneous and sequential

食たべながら話はなす。
食たべてから話はなす。
Talk while eating / Talk after eating.

Check the subject

母ははが寝ねている間あいだに、私わたしは勉強べんきょうします。
Two different people: use a time expression such as 間あいだに.
