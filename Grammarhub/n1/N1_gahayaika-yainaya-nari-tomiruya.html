N1 GRAMMAR · THE MOMENT SOMETHING HAPPENS

〜が早はやいか・〜や否いなや・〜なり・〜と見みるや

No Sooner… Than · As Soon As · The Moment… · The Moment One Judges…

Describe something happening immediately after another action, and someone acting the moment they judge a situation.

01 Meaning & Quick Comparison

Compare the meanings before choosing an expression.

014 · 〜が早はやいか

チャイムが鳴なるが早はやいか、生徒せいとたちは教室きょうしつを飛とび出だした。
No sooner had the bell rung than the students rushed out of the classroom.

184 · 〜や否いなや

彼かれは席せきに着つくや否いなや、話はなし始はじめた。
As soon as he sat down, he started talking.

121 · 〜なり

彼女かのじょは私わたしの顔かおを見みるなり、泣なき出だした。
The moment she saw my face, she burst into tears.

108 · 〜と見みるや

犯人はんにんは警察けいさつが来きたと見みるや、裏口うらぐちから逃にげ出だした。
The moment the criminal saw that the police had come, he fled through the back door.

〜が早はやいか: No sooner… than… — the next action happens almost at the same instant.

〜や否いなや: As soon as… / no sooner… than… — the next event follows immediately (formal, written).

〜なり: As soon as… / the moment… — someone does an unexpected action right after another (same subject).

〜と見みるや: The moment one judges… — someone quickly acts as soon as they see or judge the situation.

02 How to Form

〜が早はやいか

Verb dictionary form / た-form + が早はやいか + a quick action (past)

Connection example

彼かれは家いえに帰かえるが早はやいか、ゲームを始はじめた。
No sooner had he got home than he started playing games. (dictionary form)

Connection example

子供こどもたちはお菓子かしを受うけ取とったが早はやいか、全部ぜんぶ食たべてしまった。
The children ate all the sweets as soon as they got them. (た-form)

〜や否いなや

Verb dictionary form + や否いなや ・ (shortened) dictionary form + や

Connection example

ニュースが流ながれるや否いなや、問とい合あわせの電話でんわが鳴なり始はじめた。
As soon as the news was broadcast, the phones started ringing with inquiries.

Connection example

幕まくが上あがるや、会場かいじょうは拍手はくしゅに包つつまれた。
The moment the curtain rose, the hall was filled with applause. (や alone)

〜なり

Verb dictionary form + なり + an unexpected action by the same person (past)

Connection example

父ちちは帰かえってくるなり、部屋へやにこもってしまった。
As soon as my father came home, he shut himself in his room.

Connection example

彼かれはビールを一口ひとくち飲のむなり、「まずい」と言いった。
The moment he took a sip of beer, he said, “This is awful.”

〜と見みるや

Plain form / noun (the content of a judgment) + と見みるや + a quick action

Connection example

相手あいてが弱よわいと見みるや、彼かれは一気いっきに攻せめ始はじめた。
As soon as he judged that his opponent was weak, he went all out on the attack. (い-adjective)

Connection example

不利ふりと見みるや、彼かれはすぐに計画けいかくを変更へんこうした。
The moment he saw that things were against him, he changed his plan at once. (noun / な-adjective)

03 Everyday Examples

Read the Japanese examples, then check the English meaning.

〜が早はやいか
犬いぬはドアが開あくが早はやいか、外そとへ走はしり出だした。
The dog ran outside the instant the door opened.
〜が早はやいか
新商品しんしょうひんは発売はつばいされるが早はやいか、売うり切きれた。
The new product sold out as soon as it went on sale.
〜や否いなや
娘むすめは起おきるや否いなや、スマホを見みている。
My daughter looks at her phone as soon as she wakes up.
〜や否いなや
試合しあいが始はじまるや否いなや、雨あめが降ふり出だした。
No sooner had the match started than it began to rain.
〜なり
友人ゆうじんは会あうなり、会社かいしゃの愚痴ぐちを言いい始はじめた。
As soon as we met, my friend started complaining about her company.
〜なり
彼かれは手紙てがみを読よむなり、部屋へやを飛とび出だしていった。
The moment he read the letter, he dashed out of the room.
〜と見みるや
客きゃくが少すくないと見みるや、店長てんちょうは値下ねさげを決きめた。
Seeing that there were few customers, the manager immediately decided to cut prices.
〜と見みるや
雨あめが降ふりそうだと見みるや、彼かれは洗濯物せんたくものを取とり込こんだ。
The moment he judged it was about to rain, he brought in the laundry.

04 Usage Notes & Nuance

Common points

All four describe actual events that the speaker observed (past events or habits). They are not used for the speaker’s own intentions, requests, or commands (use たらすぐ for those).

〜が早はやいか／〜や否いなや

Both mean “no sooner… than.” が早はやいか stresses speed; や否いなや is formal and written. The two actions can have different subjects.

〜なり

The same person does both actions, and the second is often surprising or unusual. It is different from なりに (in one’s own way) and from なり〜なり (either… or…).

〜と見みるや

The first part is a judgment (不利ふりだ, 弱よわい, 来きた). The subject quickly reacts to that judgment.

05 Choose the Right Expression

Choose by meaning and context, not only by English translation.

〜が早はやいか

鳴なるが早はやいか飛とび出だした。
No sooner… than (speed).

〜や否いなや

着つくや否いなや話はなし始はじめた。
As soon as (formal).

〜なり

見みるなり泣なき出だした。
Same subject, unexpected action.

〜と見みるや

不利ふりと見みるや変更へんこうした。
Quick reaction to a judgment.

〜たとたん (N3)

ドアを開あけたとたん、犬いぬが出でた。
The moment (everyday).
How to choose

For everyday speech, たとたん is enough. が早はやいか stresses speed; や否いなや is written; なり is for one person’s surprising reaction; と見みるや is a reaction to a judgment.

06 Common Mistakes

〜なり · Correction

✕ 家いえに着つくなり、電話でんわしてください。
✓ 家いえに着ついたらすぐ電話でんわしてください。
Not used for requests.

〜や否いなや · Correction

✕ 起おきたや否いなや、スマホを見みる。
✓ 起おきるや否いなや、スマホを見みる。
Use the dictionary form.

〜なり · Correction

✕ 私わたしが帰かえるなり、母ははが料理りょうりを出だした。
✓ 母ははは私わたしの顔かおを見みるなり、料理りょうりを出だした。
The same person does both actions.
