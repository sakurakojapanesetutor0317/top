01 Meaning & Quick Comparison

Compare the meanings before choosing an expression.

056 · 〜だの〜だの

アイスだの、ケーキだの、そんなのばっかり食たべてるから太ふとるんだ。
You gain weight because you eat nothing but things like ice cream and cake.

185 · やれ〜やれ〜

やれ塾じゅくだ、やれスポーツクラブだと、最近さいきんの子供こどもは大人おとなより忙いそがしい。
With cram school and sports clubs, children these days are busier than adults.

190 · 〜わ〜わ

昨日きのうは一日いちにち、甥おいと姪めいの面倒めんどうを見みたが、騒さわぐわ泣なくわで大変たいへんだった。
I looked after my nephew and niece all day yesterday; they cried and made noise, and it was exhausting.

〜だの〜だの: Things like… and… — lists examples, usually with complaint or criticism.

やれ〜やれ〜: What with… and… — lists one demand or event after another; busy or annoying.

〜わ〜わ: …and… — one thing after another, often too much or bad (emotional).

02 How to Form

〜だの〜だの

Noun / い-adjective / plain form + だの、 + noun / い-adjective / plain form + だの (often + と言いう / と文句もんくを言いう)

Connection example

弟おとうとは暑あついだの眠ねむいだのと言いって、何なにもしない。
My brother says things like “It is hot” and “I am sleepy” and does nothing. (い-adjective)

Connection example

行いくだの行いかないだの、はっきりしない人ひとだ。
Going, not going — he cannot make up his mind. (plain form)

やれ〜やれ〜

やれ + noun + だ、やれ + noun + だ(と) ・ often with 忙いそがしい・うるさい・お金かねがかかる

Connection example

やれ会議かいぎだ、やれ出張しゅっちょうだと、休やすむ暇ひまもない。
What with meetings and business trips, I have no time to rest.

Connection example

年末ねんまつはやれ大掃除おおそうじだ、やれ年賀状ねんがじょうだと忙いそがしい。
At the end of the year I am busy with the big cleaning, New Year cards, and so on.

〜わ〜わ

Verb dictionary form / い-adjective + わ、 + verb / い-adjective + わ(で) ・ same verb repeated: 降ふるわ降ふるわ (a huge amount)

Connection example

財布さいふはなくすわ、電車でんしゃは遅おくれるわで、ひどい一日いちにちだった。
I lost my wallet, the train was late — what a terrible day. (verbs)

Connection example

雨あめが降ふるわ降ふるわ、一晩中ひとばんじゅう止やまなかった。
It rained and rained and did not stop all night. (same verb repeated)

03 Everyday Examples

Read the Japanese examples, then check the English meaning.

〜だの〜だの
部屋へやが狭せまいだの駅えきから遠とおいだの、文句もんくばかり言いっている。
He complains all the time that the room is small, that it is far from the station, and so on.
〜だの〜だの
服ふくだの靴くつだのに、お金かねを使つかいすぎた。
I spent too much money on things like clothes and shoes.
やれ〜やれ〜
母はははやれ勉強べんきょうしろ、やれ片付かたづけろとうるさい。
My mother keeps nagging me: study, clean up…
やれ〜やれ〜
やれ入学金にゅうがくきんだ、やれ制服代せいふくだいだと、お金かねがかかる。
Entrance fees, uniform costs — it all adds up.
〜わ〜わ
新あたらしい靴くつは痛いたいわ歩あるきにくいわで、買かわなければよかった。
The new shoes hurt and are hard to walk in — I wish I had not bought them. (い-adjectives)
〜わ〜わ
虫むしが出でるわ出でるわ、数かぞえきれなかった。
Bugs kept coming out one after another; I could not count them.

04 Usage Notes & Nuance

〜だの〜だの — critical list

It is similar to 〜とか〜とか but usually shows that the speaker is annoyed by what someone says or does. It often appears with と言いう or と文句もんくを言いう.

やれ〜やれ〜 — demands one after another

It is usually used in the form やれ〜だ、やれ〜だ: やれ comes before each item. It describes a busy or troublesome series of things. It is spoken.

〜わ〜わ — overwhelming

Two different events emphasize that bad things happened together. The same verb repeated (降ふるわ降ふるわ, 出でるわ出でるわ) shows a surprising amount. It is spoken and emotional.

05 Choose the Right Expression

Choose by meaning and context, not only by English translation.

〜だの〜だの

暑あついだの眠ねむいだの
Things like (critical).

〜とか〜とか (N4)

暑あついとか眠ねむいとか
Things like (neutral).

〜やら〜やら (N2)

洗濯せんたくやら掃除そうじやら
Many things (busy).

やれ〜やれ〜

やれ塾じゅくだ、やれ習ならい事ごとだ
One demand after another.

〜わ〜わ

騒さわぐわ泣なくわ
One bad thing after another.
How to choose

とか is neutral. だの adds criticism of what someone says. やら and やれ express being busy. わ〜わ is the most emotional and emphasizes that too much happened.

06 Common Mistakes

やれ〜やれ〜 · Correction

✕ 塾じゅくやれ、スポーツやれ
✓ やれ塾じゅくだ、やれスポーツだ
やれ comes before each item.

〜わ〜わ · Correction

✕ 雨あめが降ふったわ風かぜが吹ふいたわで大変たいへんだった。
✓ 雨あめは降ふるわ風かぜは吹ふくわで大変たいへんだった。
The dictionary form is normally used before わ, even for past events.
