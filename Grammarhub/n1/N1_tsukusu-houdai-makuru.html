N1 GRAMMAR · DOING SOMETHING TO THE FULL

〜尽つくす・〜放題ほうだい・〜まくる

Do… Completely / Exhaust… · As Much as One Likes / Without Restraint · Do… Repeatedly or Intensely

Use verb endings to say that an action is done completely, without limits, or over and over with great energy.

01 Meaning & Quick Comparison

Compare the meanings before choosing an expression.

066 · 〜尽つくす

彼女かのじょの家いえに行いったら、食たべ尽つくせないほどの料理りょうりが並ならんでいた。
When I visited her home, there was more food than we could possibly finish.

172 · 〜放題ほうだい

この店みせは食たべ放題ほうだいです。
At this restaurant, you can eat as much as you like.

173 · 〜まくる

一日中いちにちじゅう、本ほんを読よみまくった。
I spent the whole day reading books.

〜尽つくす: Do… completely / exhaust… — does something until nothing is left.

〜放題ほうだい: As much as one likes / without restraint — no limit (positive), or left uncontrolled (negative).

〜まくる: Do… repeatedly or intensely — does something again and again, with great energy (casual).

02 How to Form

〜尽つくす

Verb ます-stem + 尽つくす (食たべ尽つくす・使つかい尽つくす・知しり尽つくす・言いい尽つくす)

Connection example

彼かれはこの町まちのことを知しり尽つくしている。
He knows everything about this town.

Connection example

言葉ことばでは言いい尽つくせないほど感謝かんしゃしている。
I am more grateful than words can say.

〜放題ほうだい

Verb ます-stem + 放題ほうだい (食たべ放題ほうだい・飲のみ放題ほうだい) ・ verb たい-form + 放題ほうだい (言いいたい放題ほうだい) ・ な-adjective stem + 放題ほうだい (好すき放題ほうだい) ・ 荒あれ放題ほうだい (left to ruin)

Connection example

彼かれは会議かいぎで言いいたい放題ほうだい言いって帰かえった。
He said whatever he wanted at the meeting and left. (たい + 放題ほうだい, critical)

Connection example

子供こどもを好すき放題ほうだいにさせてはいけない。
You should not let children do whatever they like. (な-adjective stem)

〜まくる

Verb ます-stem + まくる (食たべまくる・買かいまくる・遊あそびまくる・しゃべりまくる)

Connection example

セールで服ふくを買かいまくった。
I bought clothes like crazy at the sale.

Connection example

夏休なつやすみは遊あそびまくるつもりだ。
I plan to play non-stop during the summer holidays.

04 Usage Notes & Nuance

〜尽つくす — until nothing is left

It means doing something completely: eating all the food, knowing everything, using every method. The negative potential (言いい尽つくせない) is common.

〜放題ほうだい — no limit

In shops it is positive (食たべ放題ほうだい, 飲のみ放題ほうだい). With 言いいたい放題ほうだい, 好すき放題ほうだい, やりたい放題ほうだい it is critical: someone acts without restraint. 荒あれ放題ほうだい and 伸のび放題ほうだい describe something left neglected.

〜まくる — again and again

It is casual and emphasizes repeating an action with energy. It is not used in formal writing.

05 Choose the Right Expression

Choose by meaning and context, not only by English translation.

〜尽つくす

食たべ尽つくす
Eat it all (nothing left).

〜放題ほうだい

食たべ放題ほうだい
Eat as much as you like (no limit).

〜まくる

食たべまくる
Eat and eat (energetically).

〜きる (N2)

食たべきる
Finish eating (completion).
How to choose

尽つくす and きる are about completion (尽つくす is stronger: everything, every method). 放題ほうだい means there is no limit or control. まくる is about repetition and energy.

06 Common Mistakes

〜まくる · Correction

✕ （formal report）関係者かんけいしゃに話はなしを聞ききまくった。
✓ 関係者かんけいしゃに幅広はばひろく話はなしを聞きいた。
まくる is casual.

〜放題ほうだい · Correction

✕ 好すきな放題ほうだいにする。
✓ 好すき放題ほうだいにする。
な-adjective stem + 放題ほうだい.
