N1 GRAMMAR · AS IF

〜ごとく・〜とばかりに・〜んばかりに

Like… / As Though… (Literary) · As If to Say… · As If About To… / Almost…

Describe something by comparison: in a literary way, as if someone were saying something, or as if something were about to happen.

01 Meaning & Quick Comparison

Compare the meanings before choosing an expression.

027 · 〜ごとく／〜ごとき

真夏まなつに外そとで作業さぎょうすると、汗あせが滝たきのごとく流ながれ落おちる。
When I work outside in midsummer, sweat pours down like a waterfall.

105 · 〜とばかりに

料理りょうりを食たべて、まずいとばかりに顔かおをしかめた。
After tasting the food, he frowned as if to say it was awful.

208 · 〜んばかりに

頭あたまが割われんばかりにひどい頭痛ずつうが続つづいている。
I have a severe headache that feels as if my head will split open.

〜ごとく／〜ごとき: Like… / as though… — a literary way of saying ように / ような.

〜とばかりに: As if to say… — someone’s attitude or action clearly shows what they are thinking, though they do not say it.

〜んばかりに: As if about to… / almost… — something seems as if it is about to happen (an extreme state).

02 How to Form

〜ごとく／〜ごとき

Noun + の + ごとく / ごとき + noun ・ verb dictionary form / た-form + (が)ごとく

Connection example

彼かれは何事なにごともなかったかのごとく振ふる舞まった。
He behaved as though nothing had happened. (〜かのごとく)

Connection example

前まえに述のべたごとく、この計画けいかくには問題もんだいがある。
As stated before, this plan has problems. (た-form)

〜とばかりに

Quoted words (plain form, command, volitional: 「どうだ」・帰かえれ・今いまだ) + とばかりに ・ noun + とばかりに (チャンス・好機こうき)

Connection example

彼かれは「どうだ」とばかりに胸むねを張はった。
He puffed out his chest as if to say, “How about that?” (quoted words)

Connection example

店員てんいんは早はやく帰かえれとばかりに片付かたづけを始はじめた。
The staff began cleaning up as if to say, “Go home already.” (command)

〜んばかりに

Verb ない-form stem + んばかりに / んばかりの + noun / んばかりだ (言いう → 言いわん, する → せん)

Connection example

彼女かのじょは泣なかんばかりに頼たのんできた。
She begged me, almost in tears.

Connection example

会場かいじょうは割われんばかりの拍手はくしゅに包つつまれた。
The hall was filled with thunderous applause. (んばかりの + noun)

03 Everyday Examples

Read the Japanese examples, then check the English meaning.

〜ごとく／〜ごとき
時間じかんは矢やのごとく過すぎていった。
Time flew by like an arrow.
〜ごとく／〜ごとき
彼かれは水みずを得えた魚さかなのごとく生いき生いきとしていた。
He was full of life, like a fish in water.
〜とばかりに
雨あめがやむと、今いまだとばかりに子供こどもたちが外そとに飛とび出だした。
When the rain stopped, the children rushed outside as if to say, “Now!”
〜とばかりに
相手あいてのミスをチャンスとばかりに、一気いっきに攻せめた。
Seizing the opponent’s mistake as a chance, they attacked all at once. (noun)
〜んばかりに
彼かれは「お前まえのせいだ」と言いわんばかりに私わたしをにらんだ。
He glared at me as if to say, “It is your fault.”
〜んばかりに
箱はこからあふれんばかりにおもちゃが入はいっている。
The box is so full of toys that it is almost overflowing.

04 Usage Notes & Nuance

〜ごとく — literary ように

It is classical and written. ごとく modifies verbs (like ように), ごとき modifies nouns (like ような), and ごとし ends a sentence (光陰こういん矢やのごとし). Note: 私わたしごとき can also mean “someone as humble as me.”

〜とばかりに — what the attitude shows

The words before it are what the person seems to be saying through actions. It describes other people, not oneself. The words can be a command or volitional form.

〜んばかりに — about to happen

ん is the classical negative-volitional form after the ない-stem. It describes an extreme state that is nearly reached (泣なかんばかり, 割われんばかり). 言いわんばかりに is very similar to とばかりに.

05 Choose the Right Expression

Choose by meaning and context, not only by English translation.

〜ごとく

滝たきのごとく
Like (literary).

〜ように (N3)

滝たきのように
Like (neutral).

〜とばかりに

どうだとばかりに
As if to say (attitude).

〜んばかりに

泣なかんばかりに
As if about to (state).

〜かのように (N2)

知しらないかのように
As if (contrary to fact).
How to choose

とばかりに shows the message of an action. んばかりに shows that a state is almost reached. ごとく is a literary replacement for ように.

06 Common Mistakes

〜んばかりに · Correction

✕ 泣なくんばかりに頼たのんだ。
✓ 泣なかんばかりに頼たのんだ。
ない-form stem + ん.

〜ごとく · Correction

✕ 滝たきごとく
✓ 滝たきのごとく
Noun + の + ごとく.

〜とばかりに · Correction

✕ 私わたしは「帰かえりたい」とばかりに時計とけいを見みた。
✓ 彼かれは「帰かえりたい」とばかりに時計とけいを見みた。
It describes other people.
