N1 GRAMMAR · SYMPATHY & REGRET

〜だろうに・〜ものを

Even Though… Probably (Sympathy) · If Only… (Regret / Criticism)

Show sympathy or surprise about someone’s situation, and express regret or criticism that something did not happen as it should have.

01 Meaning & Quick Comparison

Compare the meanings before choosing an expression.

064 · 〜だろうに

仕事しごとで疲つかれているだろうに、母ははは毎日まいにち料理りょうりを作つくってくれる。
Even though she must be tired from work, my mother cooks for us every day.

182 · 〜ものを

新幹線しんかんせんで行いけばいいものを、安やすいからとバスで行いくから疲つかれるんだ。
You could have just taken the bullet train, but you took the bus because it was cheap — that is why you are tired.

〜だろうに: Even though… probably… — imagines someone’s situation with sympathy, or regrets an unreal possibility.

〜ものを: If only… / although… — regret or criticism that something did not happen as it should have.

02 How to Form

〜だろうに

Plain form (な-adjective / noun: だろうに) + だろうに ・ (unreal) 〜ば〜だろうに

Connection example

忙いそがしいだろうに、わざわざ来きてくれてありがとう。
You must be busy, so thank you for coming all this way. (sympathy)

Connection example

もう少すこし早はやく気きづけば、助たすかっただろうに。
If only someone had noticed a little earlier, he probably would have been saved. (unreal possibility, regret)

〜ものを

Plain form + ものを ・ often 〜ばいいものを / 〜ばよかったものを

Connection example

言いってくれれば手伝てつだったものを、どうして黙だまっていたの。
I would have helped if you had told me — why did you stay silent? (regret / criticism)

Connection example

謝あやまればすむものを、彼かれは意地いじを張はっている。
He could settle it just by apologizing, but he is being stubborn.

03 Everyday Examples

Read the Japanese examples, then check the English meaning.

〜だろうに
一人ひとりで寂さびしいだろうに、彼女かのじょは弱音よわねを吐はかない。
She must be lonely on her own, but she never complains.
〜だろうに
教おしえてくれれば手伝てつだっただろうに。
If you had told me, I would have helped.
〜ものを
少すこし待まてばいいものを、彼かれはすぐに怒おこり出だした。
He could have waited a little, but he got angry right away.
〜ものを
早はやく病院びょういんに行いけば治なおったものを。
If only you had gone to the hospital earlier, it would have healed.

04 Usage Notes & Nuance

〜だろうに — imagining someone’s feelings

The speaker imagines another person’s hardship (tired, busy, lonely) with sympathy, and contrasts it with what the person actually does. With 〜ば, it expresses regret about something that could have happened but did not.

〜ものを — regret or criticism

It is like のに but more emotional and old-fashioned. It shows dissatisfaction that something easy or better was not done. The sentence can end with ものを itself.

05 Choose the Right Expression

Choose by meaning and context, not only by English translation.

〜だろうに

疲つかれているだろうに、作つくってくれる。
Must be…, and yet (sympathy).

〜ものを

行いけばいいものを、行いかない。
Should have…, but didn’t (criticism).

〜のに (N4)

教おしえてくれればよかったのに。
Although / if only (everyday).

〜ばよかった (N3)

早はやく行いけばよかった。
Should have (regret).
How to choose

だろうに focuses on imagining someone else’s situation; ものを expresses dissatisfaction or regret. In everyday speech, のに and ばよかった are used.

06 Common Mistakes

〜だろうに · Correction

✕ 私わたしは疲つかれているだろうに、料理りょうりを作つくった。
✓ 母ははは疲つかれているだろうに、料理りょうりを作つくってくれた。
It imagines another person’s situation.

〜ものを · Correction

✕ 早はやく行いけばいいものを、早はやく行いった。
✓ 早はやく行いけばいいものを、遅おくれて行いった。
What actually happened goes against what should have happened.
